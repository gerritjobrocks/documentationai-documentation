# Edit employee profiles

## Open profile and recognize access type

Open **Employees**, search for the person and open their profile. From staff assignment, the profile icon also leads to the person. A visible name in a candidate list does not automatically mean access to all profile data.

- **Agency pool:** The read permission **View All Employee Profiles** and the relationship to the accessible agency pool determine profile access. Read access is not edit access: changes require **Edit All Employee Profiles**; status changes require **Change Employee Status**. A person readable in the connected agency pool is not automatically editable in your own agency.
- **Application outside the pool:** For an application or assignment to a project in the agency group, a restricted, read-only profile may appear. A mere invitation or rejection does not create this access. Take the read-only profile notice seriously.
- **Customer portal/Self Service:** A profile from your own customer pool can be opened in read-only mode if Self Service access is available. The agency rating and additional documents have further visibility rights. The customer view is not agency administration.
- **Employees:** They edit their own profile within the released fields. Agency-internal status, rating and list areas are not the same interface as the personal profile.

If there is **no access**, do not switch to another profile address. Have pool membership and profile rights checked.

## Save and review changes

With **Open all tabs** or **Close all tabs**, you expand or collapse the sections together. Many simple fields save automatically after a change or when leaving the field. Training, languages, documents and absences use their own save actions. Therefore, there is no single final save step for the entire profile.

Wait for processing after changes and pay attention to error messages. If **Employee cannot be saved** appears, the input must not be treated as successfully saved. Reopen the relevant section and check it again. First name, last name, and email must not be empty. A social security number used twice may be rejected.

Required fields and changes marked for review depend on the agency configuration. Missing required information can result in the status **incomplete**; changes to an active profile can trigger the status **amended**. Orange markings are therefore not simply a new active employee. Check them before you release the status again.

## Personal details and internal assessment

**Personal details** contains the separately controllable status (where visible), first name, last name, nickname, gender, children, smoker status, date of birth with day/month/year, and the short self-introduction. Age is displayed from the date of birth. Only process sensitive information for the intended personnel process and with appropriate access.

**Change status individually:** Open **Personal details** and select the desired option for the status, such as **active** or **inactive**. Viewing the status and changing it are separate rights; to change it, you need **Change Employee Status** and an editable relationship to your own agency. The selection immediately starts its own save process, without a global save button. If an error occurs, the interface resets the selection to the previously loaded status. Wait for processing, pay attention to error messages, and reopen the profile to check the actually saved status.

- **Activate** can trigger an activation email to the person. Do not activate as a test; missing information for a connected personnel system can prevent activation.
- **Set inactive** sets the deactivation date and removes requests from your own agency for today and future days in the open, accepted, or automatically accepted states. This is **not removal from the agency pool**. Existing assignments are not reliably secured or revoked by this; check the schedule separately and clarify any required changes in the assignment process.

**Ratings** shows the agency rating with the number of underlying shifts. In the agency view, you can select a client and view its rating. The stars shown here are read-only; they are not an input field for a new rating. In the client portal, the client’s own rating is shown, and the agency rating only with the additional right **View Agency Rating**.

**Internal notes** is an agency view in the current profile, not a client notes dialog. The warning level offers **no issues**, **yellow card**, and **red card**; in addition, there is a notes field with a maximum of 1,000 characters. The text is queued for saving when you leave the field.

**Red card:** This selection sets the warning level and first triggers its saving. If the person is not yet inactive, a separate question follows asking whether they should also be set to inactive. **Yes** additionally starts the status change described above with its consequences for requests. **No** does not revoke the red card. Check warning level and status separately after reopening; the follow-up question is not a joint save/cancel confirmation for both changes.

**Do not consider:** This separate action opens a message and confirmation dialog. Check recipient, subject, and message; subject and message are required. The process is triggered only by **Send** and the additional confirmation. If there are assigned or confirmed assignments from your agency for today or later, removal from the pool is blocked. After successful execution, the person is removed from the agency pool and a notification is triggered; then check the pool. This is neither the red card nor the **inactive** selection. The assignment block in this dialog is not a guaranteed protective effect of the status change. If the sending feedback is unclear, do not resend blindly; first check the pool status and the feedback.

## Lists and client pool

**Agency lists:** Open **Employee lists** in the agency view. The three multiple selections **List**, additional list (if applicable shown as **additional list**), and **black list** (exclusion list) are separate memberships, not merely display filters. To edit them, you need the appropriate profile access and **Edit All Employee Profiles**. A hidden agency area in the client portal is not an error that should be bypassed with another link.

1. Open the desired list type, search for an existing list, and select it to add the person. You can select multiple lists.
2. Deselect an already marked entry, or remove its selection chip, to take the person out of exactly that list. This deletes neither the list nor the employee profile.
3. Only for **List** and **additional list** can you enter a new list name and press **Enter**: this creates a new list of that type and assigns the person to it immediately. For the **exclusion list** and the client pickers below, no new creation via Enter is intended here. The separate list administration is explained in the [list and filter guide](/main/en/05b_employee_lists_filters).

Every selection change saves the memberships immediately; there is no global save button and no final collective confirmation. Enter is also a write action, not merely a search.

**Client pool:** Open the section of the same name. In the agency view, its visibility requires a Self Service access. Changes to the client pool and client lists additionally require the Self Service editing right **Edit All Self Service Projects**; read access alone or a visible picker is not sufficient.

- **In the client portal**, under **client employee lists**, you select or deselect existing lists of your own client. The person must already be in your own client pool. This picker edits your own list memberships, not agency lists or the pools of other clients.
- **In the agency view** there are two separate multiple selections: under **client pool** you select a client to add the person to that client’s pool, or deselect them to remove this pool relationship. Under **client employee lists** you select or deselect existing lists of the pool clients; the entries can be distinguished as **client – list**. Therefore always check the client name as well.

These pickers also save every selection change immediately, without a global save. **Adding a person to a client pool can trigger a notification to the target client. Removing a client simultaneously removes all list memberships of this person for that client**, not just the visible pool chip. The cross-client list selection is then reloaded. Removing a single client list is, by contrast, not a removal from the client pool.

**Follow-up check:** After each change, wait for processing and note success or error, if shown. Reopen the profile and the relevant section and check the selected lists or clients. After removing a client, also check the client lists. A visible chip alone does not prove successful saving; if rejected, clarify rights and pool context, rather than triggering possible notifications by adding again.

## Appearance, images, and skills

**Appearance:** eye color, hair color, height, shoe size, shirt size, trouser width, and trouser length. Size aids are provided through comparison tables; they do not change profile data. For visible tattoos and piercings, there are yes/no entries and corresponding descriptions. Use this information only in the intended access-restricted profile process.

**Images:** Upload portrait and full-body image in the designated fields. You can open a larger view of the existing image; deleting it is a separate action. Do not confuse an image with the CV document.

**Skills:**

| Area | Inputs and procedure |
| --- | --- |
| CV | Open the existing document; if you have editing rights, upload a file or use a recording. Deletion is done via the remove action. |
| Education | **Add New**, enter type, institute, from and to. **Current education** disables the to field. The certificate can only be assigned after the entry has been created. |
| Work experience | **Add New**, choose profession from the nested profession menu, level of experience, company, from and to. **Current job** disables the to field. |
| Languages | **Add New**, select language and level of proficiency; a language certificate can be assigned to the entry. The agency may require a native language. |

The remove action for education or work experience affects the respective entry. It is not the same as removing its certificate. Therefore, check the confirmation dialog.

## Upload, replace, and remove files

Open the profile and the relevant section. Click the upload area or drag a file onto it. Images use image files; the document areas for CV, IDs, and certificates accept images, PDF, DOC, and DOCX. Wait for processing and then open the file to check it. Uploading the same file type again generally replaces the previous file; it does not create a freely named document repository.

| File | Operation and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Portrait | Upload in the profile header or under **Images**. Both places refer to the same portrait. Enlarging opens the view; deleting removes the portrait after confirmation. |
| Full-body image and four other images | Use the separate upload area for each one under **Images**. Enlarging and deleting affect only the selected image. |
| Video self-portrait | Use the video area under **Personal details**. The file selector is designed for video and 50 MB; videos longer than 30 seconds are rejected. The remove icon on the video deletes that video, not the portrait. |
| CV | Open the file under **Skills**, upload it, or use the offered photo capture. The remove icon and confirmation apply to the CV file, not to the education and work entries. |
| Education and language certificates | First create the associated entry with its required information. Then upload the file in exactly that row; another upload replaces its certificate. Removing the certificate deletes only the file reference; deleting the row removes the entire skill entry. For language certificates, use file upload instead of the camera. |
| Social security card | Upload, open, or remove after confirmation in the dedicated card field under **Work information**. The social security number is a separate text field. |
| Passport/ID card | Front and back are separate files with their own open, upload, capture, and remove actions. |
| Work permit | Front and back appear for a selected permit, except for **no permit** or **notification procedure**. An expiry date is also provided except for permit C. For Swiss nationality, this area is not available for input. |
| Driver’s license | Under **Transport information**, upload, open, or remove the front and back separately. Driver’s license categories and driving experience are not filled in automatically by this. |
| Basic agreement | In the agency context, upload or record under **Work information**; open existing agreements. The delete action applies to the selected agreement entry and asks for confirmation. Employees can read existing agreements; the client profile area does not show them. Check a replacement by reopening it before relying on the file. |

**Photo capture:** Allow the camera, select **Capture**, check the preview, if necessary **Retake**, then **Save**. For additional documents, this is initially only the file selection; finally click **Add** or **Save** in the document dialog. If a camera is missing or blocked, use file upload. For the language certificate, this alternative upload path is preferable.

**Additional documents:** The document type and a selected file are required when adding. In the edit dialog, too, a newly selected file is required in the checked version; merely changing the type and leaving the old file display in place is not enough. The remove button on an upload file that is still selected discards the selection. To actually delete a saved document, use the trash icon in its table row and confirm the dialog. Then reopen the list and check. The file type does not automatically determine whether forwarding is permitted.

## Addresses and contact

In the **Address/contact information** section, there is a main address and a second address, as well as the marking of the legal address. Use the address search or check street, house number, postal code, city, country, and, if applicable, canton individually. Any found address should be checked for completeness before saving.

The validity details of the main and secondary address each belong to the respective address. Check both separately; marking something as the legal address is not the same as a validity date. An address supplement adds to the postal information, but does not replace either the street or the city.

The contact fields include landline, mobile number with country code, email, recovery email, emergency contact and emergency phone, as well as social profiles/web address. A separate password dialog may appear for an email change; a message in the communication dialog does not change the contact address.

If configured accordingly, hints about residence/withholding tax information appear for the main or legal address. These hints describe a data-entry dependency of the platform; they do not replace a professional assessment of your personal situation.

## Availability and absences

Open **Availability**. The table shows the recorded absence periods. Use **Add** or the edit action to open the period dialog:

1. Select the type of absence and the start/end date.
2. Enter a reason if the selected type requires one.
3. For a non-plannable type, the interface does not allow future dates. After changing the type, inappropriate dates can be reset.
4. The end may not be before the start. Check the period, save, and verify the entry in the table.

**Accept**, **Reject**, and **Delete** each ask for confirmation separately. Accept sets the absence status to approved; reject sets it to rejected. Deleting removes the period. An approved absence request does not automatically cancel shifts already assigned.

Below that is the weekly hourly grid: drag/select individual time fields or select an entire day, an hour row, or all fields. **Evenings only** and **Weekends only** are selection aids for the grid. Regular availability and a concrete absence period are two different pieces of information; for an assignment, check both together with the shift conflicts.

## Work information and restricted master data

**Work information** combines social security number and card, marital status, nationality, front/back of ID card, work permit with expiry date, as well as registration, application, interview, activation, and deactivation dates. It also includes first working day, last mobile login, employee number, branch/supervisor, and configuration-dependent information on permanent employment and internal agency staff. Basic agreements have their own document area.

When Swiss nationality is selected, the information and files for the work permit are reset. Therefore, do not change nationality as a test. For calculations of working days/hours, select the offered period; this display is not a shift assignment.

The configuration-dependent **Withholding tax** section contains information on cohabitation, single parenthood, pension, church tax/religion, partner, children, cross-border commuter/residence situation, additional employment, and substitute income. Follow the dependent questions shown and enter only the information needed for your authorized personnel process. This guide does not determine a tax classification and does not provide legal advice.

**Bank information** contains IBAN, account type/number, BIC/SWIFT, bank name, bank location, and clearing number. For the IBAN, a separate change right is checked when opening. With multiple agency relationships, editing can therefore be blocked even though other profile data are editable. Do not work around a failed IBAN check by inventing values.

## Fill in dependent withholding tax information

This guide explains only the input mask, not the tax classification. Open **Work information**, check nationality, permit, marital status with **Since when?**, and the legal address. These details and the agency configuration determine whether **Withholding tax** appears. If the input condition later no longer applies, already saved answers may remain visible for the agency in grey and read-only; in your own employee profile the section may be hidden. Do not change master data just to unlock the mask.

| Field group | Input and dependency |
| --- | --- |
| General questions | Answer cohabitation, single parent, pension receipt, and church tax with yes or no in each case. If church tax is **Yes**, the religion selection appears: Evangelical Reformed, Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic, Jewish community, or none/other. **No** resets this religion selection. |
| Partner | If married or in a registered partnership, first name, last name, date of birth (day/month/year), social security number, nationality, and the offered permit selection appear. Changing to another marital status resets dependent partner details. |
| Partner address | **Same address? – No** shows address addition, street, number, postal code, city, and country; for Switzerland, canton is also shown. After a change, check the address again and do not rely on automatically transferred values. |
| Partner employment | Answer pension receipt and employment separately. If employment is **Yes**, primary/secondary employment and start and end follow. **No** resets these employment details. |
| Single parent | If **Yes**, six separate yes/no questions follow: joint household with children eligible for deduction, household with partner, household with children and partner, sole custody, joint custody with higher gross income, and household with an adult child and higher gross income. **No** to the initial question resets the follow-up answers. The labels are form questions, not confirmation of entitlement to a deduction. |
| Children | If children are entered under personal details, fill in first name, last name, date of birth, gender, nationality, social security number, and the start and end of the period designated as the child deduction for each displayed child. Check the children rows again after changing the number of children. |
| Cross-border commuter | If **Yes**, daily or weekly return is requested. With weekly return, **Jump to addresses** appears for the Swiss residential address. The button opens the address section; there is no additional active address form block here and no automatic proof of residence. Switching to daily return resets dependent internal residential address details. |
| Additional employment | If **Yes**, employment rate in percent, gross monthly salary, and the fields marked as optional for activity and employer name/address follow. **Employment rate cannot be determined** clears and locks the percentage field. **No** for additional employment resets the follow-up details. |
| Substitute income | If **Yes**, enter the monthly amount. **No** clears the amount. |

Leave edited text fields, wait for the save, and open the section again to check it. Mandatory markers depend on the configuration. If a substantive answer is unclear, clarify it with your responsible HR office instead of choosing an answer that merely removes the mandatory marker.

## Permanent employment, salary display, and field access

Under **Work information**, the authorized agency can set **Permanently employed** and **Internal agency staff**. This alone creates neither a shift nor a new salary contract. With permanent employment, the **first working day** is offered as an input only if it was still empty when loaded; otherwise it is display-only. Registration, application, activation, inactivation date, and last mobile login must be distinguished from manually editable details such as interview date. Branch office and supervisor can be preassigned or locked by a connected personnel management system.

**Generate salary:** If the action is visible with your edit access, choose month/year. The selection starts generation; only then open the **View salary** link that appears. This is not a payout and not confirmation that all payroll data has been professionally checked. To display workdays/hours, select a period and review the result fields; they are not input fields for changing assignments.

Management of permanently employed groups and time-management corrections is not accessible through an active table in this profile state. Therefore, there is no reliably usable click path here for "add group/workload/period" or for manual hour/amount corrections. The permanently employed checkbox does not unlock these tables. Have required group or time corrections clarified by the responsible administration instead of opening a hidden dialog.

**Field-specific limits:** In the customer profile, work information, withholding tax, and banking information are missing. Additional documents require separate read access; agency ratings as well. Internal agency lists and notes are not to be equated with customer lists. In addition to profile editing, banking information has a separate IBAN lock; it affects the usability of the entire banking area. BIC/SWIFT is shown when an IBAN exists without the CH prefix. The withholding tax area can also be non-editable because of additional profile/IBAN locks or its archived state. Neither an opened document nor a visible field automatically authorizes upload, deletion, or export.

## Change email address

Open **Address/contact information → email**. If the field is editable, enter the new address and leave the field. If the address differs from the loaded profile, the password dialog appears. There, enter the password of your currently signed-in account and select **Update email**; do not request the affected person's password. The eye icon only toggles password visibility.

An incorrect password or an address already used by another account triggers the error message. Only a successful response and the reopened profile confirm the change. The action changes the account and employee email, not only the recovery address. **Recovery email** is a separate profile field. If the field is read-only or the change is denied, involve the responsible administrator; do not bypass the access lock.

## Mobility and job interests

**Transport information** distinguishes:

- Public transport subscriptions: none, half-fare, GA, or regional network with network name;
- Driving license categories or no driving license, plus the front/back of the document;
- Driving experience with automatic/manual transmission and other vehicle types offered;
- Car availability: own car, car in the household, or no car; if applicable, body type/size;
- Car-sharing subscriptions from the available selection.

For groups marked as mandatory, at least one suitable option must be selected. **No driving license** or **no car** is a substantive answer, not the same as an empty mandatory field. Under **Job interests**, you select the offered products and job types; this does not yet assign the person to a project.

## Additional documents and calendar

Under **Additional documents**, open **Add New**, select a document type and a file, and confirm **Add**. Both details are required. Document types already used are not offered again when adding. To replace one, use **Edit** on the existing document, check the current file, and select a new file; the current edit dialog requires this selection. In the customer portal, this area requires **View Further Documents**; a visible file link does not mean the profile is editable.

**Your assignment calendar** and **Imported assignment history** are different areas. Imported history is a transferred retrospective, not proof of a currently assigned or confirmed shift. For current assignments, go to [personnel assignment in step 4](/main/en/04b_staff_allocation).

## Read the assignment calendar

Open **Your assignment calendar**. Switch between **List**, **Week**, and **Month**. In week/month view, use the arrows or select the desired period in the selection field. In the list, scrolling loads earlier or later entries.

The legend distinguishes past assignments, future assignments, and unavailability. In week view, the regular availability grid is also shown. Hover over an assignment to see its details: depending on the released data, project, employer/customer, role, location, status, and planned times; for past assignments, also rating and actual start/end time. Missing actual times are not proof that work was performed.

Clicking a released assignment opens step 4 of the project for administration in a new tab and focuses the relevant assignment. PPC assignments lead to the separate PPC view. Employees do not receive this project link. Unreleased assignments can appear in the customer context only as unavailability without a name or project details. Such a block is not automatically an absence request entered by the person. The calendar does not change any assignment; you edit absences in the **Availability** section, where year and status narrow the table.

## Password and messages

**Reset employee password** requires the corresponding permission. The dialog requires a temporary password with at least eight characters, uppercase/lowercase letters, and a number. Check the person first; changing a password is not an invitation to an assignment. Do not share passwords through general employee lists.

**Send notification** is addressed to this single person. Note the [differences between communication dialogs](/main/en/05d_employee_communication), especially the restriction of the single-profile dialog for email and SMS.
