---
title: Find and Accept Shifts
description: How to find and confirm shifts in the employee app.
---

In the app you see all available shifts you are qualified for.

## Find Shifts

- Open the "Shifts" section
- Filter by status, date, or location
- Tap a shift to see details

## Accept a Shift

- Read the shift details
- Tap "Accept" or "Apply"
- Wait for confirmation by the planner
