## Open employee pool

Open **Employees** in the main menu. The dashboard shows the employee pool in the selected agency context, not the applications of a single project. Viewing the overview requires permission to view all employee profiles. List management and status changes have separate permissions; a visible profile does not automatically mean edit rights.

## Tabs, search and selection

- **Active**, **Applications**, **Changed**, **Expired**, **Incomplete** and **Inactive** narrow the list by employee status. These statuses are not the same as “invited”, “assigned” or “confirmed” for an assignment.
- **Filter** opens the detailed [filter selection](/main/en/05b_employee_lists_filters). Switching to a status tab resets the filter selection for that tab.
- Enter the name in the search field. The overview reloads while you type. A single search term is searched in, among other things, first name, last name, email and mobile number; for first and last name, the first two words are assigned to these two name fields.
- Open a profile via the employee row. The table loads page by page and is initially sorted by last name in ascending order.
- Before any bulk action, check the **number of selected people**: the selection can include the entire filtered result set across multiple pages. Not only the visible rows are affected. Deselect people you do not want.

When a filter is set, the active criteria appear above the table. Use the **x** on a criterion to remove it; then check the new number of results.

## Bulk actions and activation

Next to the row count, **Export**, **Send message** and — with list rights — **Save as new list**, **Save as new additional list** and the action for a new blacklist appear when items are selected. **Edit lists** opens the existing list management. Details are provided under [Lists and filters](/main/en/05b_employee_lists_filters) and [Communication](/main/en/05d_employee_communication).

**Set selected to active** appears only with status-change permission and when the filter for edited profiles is active. Check the selection and profile changes, open the action and confirm the prompt. The response distinguishes between successful and failed activations. In the event of partial success, check the remaining profiles individually; do not blindly run the entire selection again. Activation is not an assignment confirmation; in connected personnel management systems, missing details or a rejected transfer can prevent the status change.

## Not considered is not the same as setting to inactive

The **Not considered** action opens its own message dialog. Check the subject and message, choose **Send** and confirm only after checking. **Cancel** or closing the dialog discards the not-yet-sent decision. Assigned or confirmed assignments from today onward prevent this action.

The confirmed action removes the person from the relevant agency pool and records the decision as “not considered”. It is not merely a switch to the **Inactive** tab and does not delete the identity across all organizations. The notification is handed off separately for processing; removal from the pool is not proof that the person received the message.

## Invite employees to register

These instructions are for planners or administrators who want to **add new employees to the agency pool**. You need access to your agency’s agency profile. The registration link is not a link to a single project.

1. Open **Settings** for your agency in the left navigation.
2. Look for **the agency name next to the logo at the very top of the page**. The **Copy registration link** button is located right there.
3. Click **Copy registration link** and first paste the copied link into your message draft. Check the portal and agency before passing it on to the relevant person.
4. Ask the person to open this link and register for the agency shown.
5. After registration, check the employee and their status in the pool. Successful registration does not mean the person is activated, invited to an assignment or already assigned.

### Where exactly in Settings?

This refers to the **agency profile**, identifiable by the agency name and logo at the very top. For orientation, its browser address starts with `/agency/profile/`, followed by your agency ID. The button is directly next to the agency name, not in a project submenu and not under an individual employee profile.

There may be a **different settings area** with **Invite members**, **Invite customers** and **Security**. **Invite members** invites administrative users; **Invite customers** invites customer users. These forms are **not employee registration**. The agency profile’s user management is also for administrators and project leaders, not for the employee registration link.

**The link uses the address of the currently open portal.** In a branded portal, it may differ from another bejoo portal access. Copy the provided link unchanged; do not construct it yourself from the agency ID or a guessed agency name.

### What the registering person fills in

The person first checks that the correct agency is displayed. The web form requires **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Date of birth** (day/month/year), **Password** and consent to the displayed platform and agency terms. They enter their mobile number with the appropriate country code; the code is initially **+41**. The password needs at least **eight characters**, one lowercase letter, one uppercase letter, a number and a special character.

This is followed by verification with a **confirmation code** and completion of the registration. If successful, the app download is offered. If an account already exists, a password confirmation may appear in the agency context to add the person to the pool; otherwise the notice leads to login. Do not create a second identity with a different email as a precaution.

### If the link or registration does not work

- **Button missing:** First check whether you are really on the agency profile at the agency name and logo. Then have an authorized administrator check access to the agency profile. Another visible settings area does not guarantee this access.
- **Wrong agency or invalid registration page:** Use the link freshly copied from the correct portal. If the agency/terms context is missing, contact the agency administration instead of guessing a link.
- **Form reports an error:** Check required fields, email, date of birth, mobile number with country code, password rules and the displayed consents. Enter the code only in the registration flow; do not share it with support.
- **Registered, but no assignment visible yet:** Check registration, activation, a matching assignment request and assignment separately. If **Automatic invitation upon activation** is enabled in the project, this process also requires suitable open future staffing needs and a matching role filter; it does not confirm a booking.

If you want to **invite existing employees to a specific assignment**, use [Publication and assignment](/main/en/04a_publication_and_allocation) instead.
