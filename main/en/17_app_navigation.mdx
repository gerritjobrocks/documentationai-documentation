## Getting around the app

Open the menu at the top left to switch between your profile, assignment lists, and calendar. At the bottom of the screen, in the view shown, you will also find **Dashboard**, **My Next Jobs**, **Job Proposals**, and **Assigned Jobs**.

This guide is based on the provided English-language app view. The English labels are therefore shown alongside the explanations. The screenshots show a specific profile and data state, not all possible views.

## Dashboard and job lists

The dashboard separates several areas:

- **My Next Jobs – in the next 48h:** next assignments in the displayed 48-hour period.
- **Job Proposals:** assignment proposals.
- **applied:** applications for assignments.
- **assigned:** assigned jobs.
- **accepted:** accepted assignments.

In the example, these lists are empty. Messages such as **no next jobs**, **no job proposals** or **no assigned jobs** refer to the respective area. They are not proof that your account is not working.

At the top is the **Search for jobs** search field with a filter icon next to it. Which filters are available after opening it is not shown in this view.

### Open a specific list

1. Open the menu at the top left.
2. Select the desired entry, for example **Job Proposals**, **Applied Jobs**, **Assigned Jobs** or **My Jobs**.
3. Check the respective list area. The **My Next Jobs** and **Job Proposals** views are also accessible via the bottom navigation.

An application, assignment and acceptance are differently labeled states. Do not treat them as interchangeable confirmations. The empty lists shown here contain no button to apply or accept; this guide therefore does not describe a new confirmation process.

## Further areas in the menu

- **Profile:** personal details, contact information, qualifications and other profile areas.
- **Job calendar:** dated overview with assignment cards and status information.
- **Rejected Jobs:** menu item for rejected assignments.
- **Past Jobs:** past assignments and their detailed view.
- **Notification History:** menu item for the notification history.
- **Switch Language:** language selection.

The content of **Rejected Jobs** and **Notification History** is not shown in the views provided.

## Select language

1. Open the side menu.
2. Select **Switch Language**.
3. Select **English**, **Deutsch** or **Français**. A check mark identifies the selected language in the view shown.

## If you get stuck

If there are no proposals, also check the notes under **Profile**. In the profile view shown, the app prompts you to complete required fields and points out that invitations may not arrive if the profile is incomplete. A complete profile is still no guarantee of an assignment.
