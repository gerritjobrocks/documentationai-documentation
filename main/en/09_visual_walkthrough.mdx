## Process for planners

1. [Find project](/main/en/02_projects_dashboard): check status, search, and filters.
2. [Create project](/main/en/03_create_project): **Projects → Create project**, enter required information, and assign days to each location.
3. [Plan project](/main/en/04_project_planning): check roles, shifts, breaks, requirements, and filters separately.
4. [Choose publication](/main/en/04a_publication_and_allocation): decide on publication only in step 3. **Assign without publication** is an active action; after that, check the status in step 4.
5. [Have new employees register](/main/en/05_employees_dashboard#invite-employees-to-register): **settings**, at the very top by the agency name beside the logo, **copy registration link**. Do not confuse this with a mission request.
