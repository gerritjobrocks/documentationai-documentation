## Job calendar – get an overview of assignments in the calendar

Open **Job calendar** in the side menu. The calendar view shown arranges assignment cards by date. At the top, the labels **past**, **future** and **unavailable** indicate past, future and unavailability.

An assignment card shows the following in the example view:

- task and project name,
- organization or agency and client,
- assignment location,
- time period,
- status labels, for example **past** and **accepted**.

Check date, time, location and status together. **accepted** and **past** describe different aspects: an assignment can be accepted and past at the same time. The legend at the top alone does not prove a filter function; operation of such a filter is not assumed here.

## Past Jobs – read details of past assignments

The **Past Jobs** menu item leads to the past assignments area. The screenshots provided already show the **Past Job** detail page, not the previous selection step in the list.

In this detail view, the assignment name as well as organization and location appear at the top. Below that follow **Total Wages**, **Total Expenses** and **Grand Total**. Further down, individual shift data with a time period and **Payment status** are listed.

### Understand timestamps

Under **Timestamps**, the date and time appear for the existing entries:

- **clock in:** clock in.
- **pause:** start of a break.
- **resume:** continue after the break.
- **clock out:** clock out.

In one of the shifts shown, **Total** also appears with a duration. Check the timestamps if a time entry does not match your assignment, and report discrepancies to your responsible organization.

This view shows times already recorded. It does not show how you start a time entry, correct it, or have it approved retrospectively. The existing [time tracking guide](./16_time_tracking) is linked separately; its full functional description was not rechecked by these screenshots.

### Classify wages, supplements and expenses

Under **Wages & Wage Supplements**, a note such as **according to the employment contract** may appear. **Expenses** shows **no information** in the example.

A displayed **0.00**, missing expense information or **Payment status: in process** are not confirmation of a completed payout. If you have questions, refer to your payslip or your organization’s information. The screenshots show no incoming bank payment and no final payroll approval.

## Availability is a separate profile area

General availability and absence periods can be found under **Profile → General Availability**. The calendar marking **unavailable** and the profile fields belong to different views. An automatic synchronization or change to existing assignments has not been verified based on the screenshots.
