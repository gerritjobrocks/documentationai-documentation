## How do I create a project?

Open **Projects → Create project** on the left. At minimum, a translated project name with no more than 100 characters, project type, customer, in agency mode **Project Manager or HR Manager**, and at least one location with a date are required. Every added location needs its own days. Save with **Next** and check the customer confirmation. [Complete guide](/main/en/03_create_project).

## How do I invite employees?

For **new employees**, open the agency profile via **Settings** and click **Copy registration link** at the very top next to the agency name and logo. Share the copied link. For **existing employees and a specific assignment**, use step 3 of the project. [Registration](/main/en/05_employees_dashboard#invite-employees-to-register) · [Publish assignment](/main/en/04a_publication_and_allocation).

## Where do I find the registration link in settings?

On the **agency profile**, right at the top next to the agency name and logo. The button is called **Copy registration link**. The browser address starts with `/agency/profile/`. Do not look for it in the other settings section **Invite members** or **Invite customers**; those are invitations for administrative or customer users. [Location and permissions](/main/en/05_employees_dashboard#where-exactly-in-settings).

## Are registration, activation, and assignment request the same thing?

No. Registration concerns the account and the agency assignment, activation concerns the employee status in the pool. An assignment request concerns a specific job offer; assignment or self-booking concerns a specific shift. [Differences](/main/en/04a_publication_and_allocation#distinguish-between-four-processes).

## Why can’t I get any further when creating a project?

Check the name, project type, customer, and the required contact. Open each location and check its days. For **cancellation by employee**, the deadline must be at least two days. In self service, **Project Manager** is a required customer contact. [Error check](/main/en/03_create_project#if-next-does-not-work).

## What is the difference between role, shift, and requirement?

The role describes the function at the location; the shift describes its working time. The requirement specifies the number of people needed per shift and date. Creating roles does not automatically set the requirement and does not assign anyone. [Planning](/main/en/04_project_planning).

## Why does a new role show no suitable employees?

A new role starts without an applied filter. Open **Filter**, check the criteria, and click **Apply selected filters**. If there are still 0 matches, check lists, exclusions, and availability; in self service, also check the customer pool. [Role filter](/main/en/04_project_planning#determine-suitable-employees-with-the-role-filter).

## Does clicking Next in planning already publish jobs?

No. **Next** in step 2 takes you to step 3. There you select and confirm **Publish jobs** or **Assign without publication**. The latter is also an active action and not draft saving. [Publication and assignment](/main/en/04a_publication_and_allocation).

## Does switching Push off mean no one sees the offer?

No. With **Publish jobs**, offers can also be published without Push. Subject and message are ignored, as noted. [Sending options](/main/en/04a_publication_and_allocation#publish-jobs-now).

## Which template do I need?

Project templates for project setup, role templates for roles at the location, filter templates for selection criteria, and notification templates for texts. Always check new days and requirements; for standalone role templates, set cost center and department again. [Template families](/main/en/06_templates_and_reuse).
