Time tracking in the bejoo app is simple and digital.

## Track Time

- Tap "Start time tracking" at shift start
- Tap "Stop time tracking" at shift end
- Or: the planner records times centrally

## On-site Check-in

- Scan QR code at the shift location
- GPS check-in (if enabled)
- Manual confirmation by supervisor
