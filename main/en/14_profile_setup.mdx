A complete profile significantly increases your chances of getting shifts.

## Important Profile Fields

- **Personal data** (name, address, date of birth)
- **Qualifications** (certificates, experience)
- **Availability** (when can you work?)
- **Documents** (work permit, ID)

Keep your profile up to date so planners can find you faster.
