# Self Service: Permissions and customer employee pool

## Self Service and orders are separate permissions

In the customer portal, **Orders** is the ordering process; **Self Service** is your own project planning. A visible menu item does not mean that you may perform every action in it. The available functions are configured by customer role.

These rights are granted separately for Self Service:
- **Create Self Service project**
- **View all Self Service projects** and **View own Self Service projects**
- **Edit all Self Service projects** and **Edit own Self Service projects**
- **Delete & cancel Self Service project**

For customer users, “own” means: you are stored as the responsible client buyer of the Self Service project. That is not automatically every order you have ever created. For agency users, the creator is used for this ownership check. In addition, the project must belong to the appropriate customer/agency area. Self Service rights do not grant blanket edit access to normal orders.

Open **Self Service** in the main menu. The submenu for project creation or loading a template appears only with the create permission. The basic workflow is explained in [Create project](./03_create_project.mdx), [Project planning](./04_project_planning.mdx), and [Publication and assignment](./04a_publication_and_allocation.mdx). Note the Self Service differences there; a saved project or a loaded template is not yet proof of publication or of a delivered invitation.

If an action is missing, have exactly the required permission and responsibility for the relevant project checked. Reloading multiple times does not extend permission. The [customer roles matrix](./06e_agency_client_portal.mdx#client-roles-separate-columns-and-conflict-confirmation) describes where rights and conflicts between order and Self Service functions are managed.

## Edit a customer's employee pool

**Check first:** Moving is already a save action and can trigger emails. Do not use the pool as an informal selection for later.

1. In the agency, open **Customers → desired customer → Employee pool**. The tab appears when Self Service is available. Save a new customer first before editing its pool.
2. Active employees who are not yet in the customer pool are on the left; active employees in the customer pool are on the right. Filter the visible entries by name or email.
3. Move the desired people to the right. The assignment is requested immediately; the general **Save** button in the customer profile is not needed for this.
4. To remove them, move the people back to the left. This also requests the removal immediately. Open the tab again and check whether the people are on the expected side.

**Permissions:** Visibility of the tab and permission to change it are different. The permission **Edit all Self Service projects** is required for these pool changes; a pure view right or only the right for own projects is not sufficient for this pool action.

**Notifications:** For people actually added, an email is triggered for each person and each configured customer recipient. If no recipients are stored for the customer, no agency recipient list is used as a fallback. The [event recipients](./06e_agency_client_portal.mdx#event-recipients-for-a-client) are configured separately. Successful pool assignment is not delivery confirmation of the email.

**If someone is missing:** Active, not deleted employees from the agency area are offered. The left candidate list is limited to 200 entries; the visible name/email filter filters these already loaded entries. A missing result therefore does not prove that the person does not exist. Have the status and pool assignment checked specifically. After an error message or unclear feedback, do not trust only the moved screen row; reload.

Pool assignment does not assign the person to any specific shift yet. Removing them from this pool is not, in itself, a cancellation of existing shift assignments.

## Self-Service person list: entry point and rights

The following guide concerns **Employees** in the Self-Service customer area and **My lists**, not the separate [customer portal personnel overview](./07e_client_portal.mdx). List management assigns people to customer lists; it does not plan a shift and does not send an invitation.

As a customer user, open **Employees** in the main menu. Access requires at least one of the six Self Service permissions mentioned above. **View/edit own Self Service projects** can open the area, but does not automatically allow list changes.

| Process | Difference you should check |
|---|---|
| Load people and customer lists | Self Service access and assignment to customer and agency. The people overview loads active people from the customer pool, not an agency population you can freely choose. |
| **Save as new list** / **My lists** in the people overview | The buttons appear with the list management right (original name **Manage Employee Lists**). |
| Save new list, rename, delete list, add or remove member | The actual save check requires **Edit all Self Service projects**. The right for own projects alone is not sufficient here; the action does not belong to an individual project. |
| Deleting a list | This is a list change, not the right to delete/cancel a Self-Service project and not the deletion of a person. |
| Export and message | Separate active buttons; do not equate this with the visibility right of the two list buttons. Profile column rights are not a guarantee for export. |

In the list editor, the actions there are available without the same additional button hiding. A visible button therefore does not prove successful save permission. If access is denied, have the specific permission, your customer/agency assignment, and the selected customer list checked. **My lists** means lists for this customer, not exclusively lists you created personally. Do not assume department rights for orders are additional people filters: there is no department selector in either of these overviews.

The agency manages the customer pool via the customer profile described above. Customer lists and agency lists remain different list types; a list with the same name is not automatically the same list. Import, creation of new people, sedcard generation, and bulk activation are not integrated into these two overviews. Do not use **Save as new list** as a substitute for those.

## Search, sort, and check the selection of people

1. Enter a search term in the **Search Employee** field. Every keystroke reloads the first page of results. A single search part is checked against first name, last name, email, mobile number, and employee number. For multiple parts separated by spaces, the first is used as the first name and the second as the last name; this is not an arbitrary full-text search.
2. The table shows checkboxes, photo, first name, last name, age, first address as place, mobile number, and email. Use the sortable headings and the page selector to change the display, not the profile. If sorting fails, especially for calculated age, switch back to first or last name and reload; an empty table can also be a failed query.
3. At the start, all matching people are considered selected, not only the first page. Individual checkmarks remove people from the selection. Before a bulk action, check search, filter, list, and deselections together.
4. **Watch the header checkbox:** If no people have been deselected yet, a click removes only the currently loaded rows from the selection. Other pages remain selected. If deselections already exist, the next click resets all deselections. It is not a reliable button for “select nobody in the entire pool.”

The selection count is only an indicator: deselections remain when you change the search or filter and are not reliably reduced to the new set of results. Do not rely on the number alone. In the list editor, selecting or clearing a list resets search and deselections; check again afterward. The checkmarks are selection state, not saved list membership.

The overview enforces active employees in the customer pool and takes the customer-side exclusion list into account, unless this is explicitly included. **Not every follow-up action uses exactly the same search conditions:** New list and message retain the search text, Excel does not. In addition, the overview adds the customer reference for exclusion separately; list creation, messages, and export do not add it in the same way. Therefore, visible results and the follow-up result are not generally the same. Check a newly saved list or file before further use.

If people are missing, first clear the search, check the filter and the selected list, then have administration check the active status and existing pool membership. If a loading error is shown, do not assume deletion.

## Open filters and apply them to the people overview

Open **Filter**, change the required criteria, and wait for the result calculation. With **Apply selected filters**, you transfer the criteria to the people overview; it then reloads page one. The x closes the dialog via its reset path and is not the apply button. Changing a filter does not save employee profiles and does not create a customer list.

In the customer context, **Customer pool** offers the customer's own lists with the optional condition “on all selected lists”. The three agency list types and the marketplace switch are not equivalent additional list filters here. Agency rating and other documents have their own visibility rights. Changing the location is not enabled in this view.

For the individual filter fields and the result paging list, see [Lists and employee filters](./05b_employee_lists_filters.mdx#filter-select-and-apply). Pay explicit attention there to the customer view: internal agency notes, imported job history, images, work information, bank details, and job interests are not customer filter areas. Loading a filter template is not list creation. In particular, the explanation of a CV filter is not proof that a CV was uploaded.

**Before a follow-up action:** Check the people overview again after applying. The result display in the filter and the checkboxes in the overview are different states; existing deselections in the overview are not automatically cleared when applying. An unchecked filter result count is therefore not a recipient list.

## Create a new customer list or copy one from an existing list

1. Narrow the people in **Employees** and check the deselections. In the list editor, either choose an existing list as the starting point or leave the list selection empty for the pool.
2. Select **Save as new list**. The dialog is called **Save as list**; the name field may show the original placeholder **List name**. Enter a unique name, preferably 2 to 50 characters. The field contains these length requirements; the save action itself checks in particular that something remains after outer spaces are removed. An already existing name is rejected during creation.
3. Click **Save**. While the request is in progress, the button is locked. The x closes without this save action. There is no automatic saving while typing here.
4. After a successful response, the dialog closes. In the people overview, open **My lists** for verification and select the new list. In the list editor, the lists are reloaded and the returned new list is selected directly; search and deselections are reset in the process.

A new member list is saved from the people determined at the time of the action, not from a continuously updated filter rule. If you copy one, the source list remains. An empty result set can also create an empty list. Duplicate names are checked when creating the list; this does not imply the same duplicate check when renaming later.

If the name is missing, the dialog stays open and shows an error. If a request fails, the button becomes available again; the dialog remains open for correction. If the response is unclear, first check **My lists** before creating the same list again. A saved list is neither a pool assignment nor a shift assignment or message.

## Select, rename, and delete My Lists

Open **My lists** and choose a customer list at the top via **Search/select list**. The name search in the selection field searches the loaded list names. Without selecting a list, the active customer pool is shown. The x only clears the selection and reloads the pool; it does not delete any list. When changing or clearing the selection, person search and deselections are reset.

**Rename:** Select a list and click the pencil (**edit list**, if the translation is missing). In the dialog, **list name** shows the current name. Change it and click **Save**. An empty name is rejected. While the request is in progress, the input, **Cancel**, and **Save** are disabled. After success, the dialog closes and the list selection is updated. **Cancel** discards the unsaved change. If an error occurs, the dialog remains open; after an unclear response, reload the list and check the name.

**Delete list:** The trash can next to the list name (**delete list**) opens a confirmation prompt. Decline if you only wanted to clear the view. Only confirming requests the deletion. The selected list and its memberships are deleted, not people, the client pool, or shift assignments. After success, the selection is cleared, the pool is shown, and the list selection is reloaded. There is no undo button here. If an error occurs, do not conclude from individual rows disappearing that the deletion was successful; reload the list.

The **Employees** link takes you back to the people overview. Switching views does not replace a successful save response. No general “save all” button bundles these changes: renaming, creating a list, confirmed deletion, and adding a member are separate save actions.

## Add people to a list and check removal

First select the correct list in **My lists**. On the right, **Employee by name or email** appears. Type part of a first name, last name, or email and click the matching result. The click requests membership immediately, without another save button. After success, the member table and the offered people are reloaded. A person who already belongs to the list is not offered there again.

**If someone is missing:** The selection loads at most 200 active, not deleted agency pool members who are not yet in the chosen list. The text input filters these loaded results; in this interface it does not start a new server-side search. If no result appears, have pool membership and status checked. Existing membership is a prerequisite: this process does not add anyone to the client pool anew.

**Known limitation when removing:** The table provides a remove icon for each person. In the reviewed version, however, this action does not pass the person ID in the form expected by the list editor. Therefore, this click does not request a removal; no follow-up confirmation prompt appears either. The visible action is not proof of a removed membership. Do not click repeatedly or delete the whole list as a substitute.

As a separate path, you can open the person profile and change the client list selection in **Client pool**, provided you have the required permission; see the profile section below. After each change, reselect the relevant list and check the membership. In case of errors, the previously saved state is authoritative, not just a local selection marker. Removing a list membership does not deactivate a person and does not remove them from the client pool.

## Choose Excel columns and verify the actual export

1. First check the filters or the selected list and any deselections. Select **Export**.
2. Select the required additional columns: employee ID, address, email, mobile number, age, language, shoe size, upper-body size, driving licence, last assignment, number of assignments, roles, employee lists, employee number, and last login. These checkboxes select export fields, not people. A status field is not included here. When reopened within the same view, the field checkboxes remain selected.
3. Click **Export Excel**. While the request is in progress, the button is disabled. If the response returns a file path, the file opens in a new window and the dialog closes. If no window appears, check the browser’s download/pop-up settings. The x closes the dialog without exporting.
4. Before sharing the file, compare it with the intended number of people and the required columns. If an error message appears, the button is enabled again; without a returned file path, a successful download has not yet been confirmed.

**Important:** Free text search is not passed to the export from either the people overview or the list editor. A screen limited to one name can therefore export more people. Use a controlled client list or the appropriate filters and check the file. The export respects deselections; it is neither a save of the list nor a message sent to the people.

The details for assignments, roles, last assignment, and client lists are determined for the client context. This is not a promise that all export columns follow the same visibility rules as the profile. Also note the differences described above between the overview and follow-up actions in exclusion cases. Further reading: [Self-Service: Export and messages](./05b_employee_lists_filters.mdx#self-service-export-and-messages).

## Send a message to the verified selection of people

1. Check the filter, selected list, search text, and individual deselections. Open **Send message**. When opened, subject and text are cleared and **Push Notification** is preselected.
2. Choose **Push Notification** or **Email**. Fill in **Subject** and the message. Both must be filled in for the action. In these two dialogs, there is no active SMS switch, no message template loader, and no button to attach a file.
3. Check the selection before **Send**: unlike with Excel, the search text is passed along. In the list editor, the selected client list is also used. Without selecting a list, the starting set is the pool. The differences to the visible overview in exclusion cases also apply here.
4. **Send** starts the message process immediately; there is no additional confirmation prompt. While the request is in progress, the button is disabled. The x closes the dialog without this sending action.

After a successful HTTP response, the dialog closes. If the explicit recipient count is zero, “no employees selected” is reported. A success message or counted recipients do not prove delivery: Push is queued, and email uses the agency sending system and requires its complete email configuration. A client SMTP setting is not a verified substitute here for missing agency configuration.

**If there is an error, do not send again blindly.** Individual recipients may already have been processed. Even a technical response without a reliable recipient count can lead to a misleading success message. Therefore check the feedback and the actual receipt with the intended recipient; if the response is unclear, have administration or support check the sending status before retrying. A message from the overview is not the same path as communication from an individual profile.

## Person profile: client lists and automatic save actions

The person link opens the existing employee profile in the self-service context. This is not a new profile and not a switch to the separate customer portal personnel ratings overview. The actual profile rights and the client context still apply; a visible or greyed-out area alone is not permission to make changes.

**Change a person’s client lists:** Open **Client pool** in the profile. The multi-select there contains the client lists and the current memberships. Add a list or remove a selected list. Each change immediately requests the saving of the complete desired client list selection for that person. There is no general save button afterward and no additional confirmation prompt. Existing pool membership and **Edit All Self Service Projects** are required. Clearing the selection removes this customer’s memberships, not the person from the pool. After an error, the local selection is not automatically reset to the confirmed server state: reopen the profile and then check the list.

**Do not confuse this with a hidden field:** A similarly named client list selector within the agency area **Employee lists** is not accessible to customer users. Use the actually visible **Client pool** area. Likewise, the customer’s own internal note in the reviewed profile is embedded under an area hidden from customers; here it is not an operable automatic save path, even though the software contains a save function for it.

General profile autosave is not the same path for customer viewers as the active client list selection. The [profile guide](./05a_employee_profiles.mdx) describes additional profile areas and their limitations; do not transfer agency actions wholesale to your customer view. Import, sedcard, or individual communication are not additional actions of the two person tables described here.

## Self-service filters in detail and table options

### Getting started, default values, and applying

This guide supplements the people overview **Employees → Filter** in Self Service. **My lists** does not have its own filter dialog: there, the client list selected at the top together with the person search determines the table. Filters from the people overview are not automatically transferred into the list editor.

When the people overview is opened fresh, **active** is preset; a status selector is not shown in this filter. Lists, date limits, attributes, and languages are initially empty, yes/no checkboxes are off, and both distance sliders are at 0. Neither a project nor a default assignment location is taken from the customer settings. The search remains within your own client pool and the agency context. The dialog is not generally read-only: in addition to search criteria, it contains its own save and sending actions. A greyed-out area is not a promise about permissions.

1. Open **Filter** and expand the desired section. Multiple sections may be open at the same time. Choose specific criteria; several different sections restrict the result set together.
2. After making changes, wait for the new result display. Number fields sometimes only trigger recalculation when leaving the field. Select date values via the calendar and then check the display. Remove no-longer-needed rows completely instead of leaving an empty criteria row in place.
3. The number **selected / matching** opens the result side list. The name search there searches only first or last names in the loaded results. **Select all** applies to all filter results, not just the rows currently visible by name. **Deselect all** switches to explicit individual selection; then check the desired people.
4. **Apply selected filters** recalculates and then loads page one of the people overview. This is not a save as a template, client list, or profile change. Individual deselections in the filter and the separate deselections in the overview take effect one after the other; the overview keeps its own existing deselections. The display in the filter is therefore not a reliable count of later export or message recipients.

**Close and reset are different:** the x closes without the apply step and without immediately reloading the overview. Still, do not rely on everything being discarded: directly changed criteria can remain in the overview’s memory and affect the next search. For a controlled restart, use **Clear filters**, set the desired criteria again, and choose **Apply selected filters**; then also check the overview deselections. Reset removes criteria and individual filter selections, restores active and distance 0, and is not a deletion of saved templates or lists. A role/client reference taken from a template can remain. If the results are inexplicable, reopen the people overview and start without a template.

For languages, the level is a specific level, not a minimum level. For mother tongue, the certificate selection disappears; certificate values already set can still apply. Reset them before switching or remove the language row and create it again.

The similarly labeled customer list field within the agency list area is also not a second accessible selector. The rights for agency rating and further documents control the display; this does not imply any general API security guarantee.

### Templates and lists directly in the filter

**Load template:** Click **Load template**, search via **Search template**, and choose a name. This closes the template dialog and recalculates the filter, but does not yet apply it to the overview via the Apply step. For customer users, templates from their own customer are loaded. The template contains not only criteria, but also saved individual selections/deselections. When loading, numerous agency fields are removed; inactive statuses are set to active, with a warning if necessary. Even so, a template is not a guaranteed empty customer filter: hidden residual values, the starting address, or role reference can be carried over. Check the selection and, if there are unexplained restrictions, start fresh without this template.

**Save as template:** Open the action, enter a name (field limit 100 characters; the placeholder may be **Name your template**) and save. The request requires template edit rights or the corresponding Self-Service edit right; a visible button is not enough. A successful save stores the filter body and then runs the Apply path. If the subsequent hit calculation fails, that does not necessarily mean the template was not saved: first reload it and check. If the name already exists, a prompt to overwrite appears. **Do not overwrite blindly here:** the checked overwrite path does not reliably restrict same-named templates to your own customer. Decline the prompt, use a new unique name, and involve administration if a correction is needed. The x next to the name only ends name entry; it does not delete a saved template.

**Save as customer employee list:** With **Manage Employee Lists**, this additional action is also available directly in the filter. The fact that the people overview has its own list button does not hide the action in the filter. Enter a name with at least two characters (field limit 50; possible placeholder **Name your list**) and choose the save action with the same name. What is saved is the currently server-determined set of members taking the filter selection into account, not a rule that grows later. The free person search and separate deselections in the overview are not part of this filter-dialog save path. Saving additionally requires the Self-Service edit right explained above. After success, reopen **My lists** and check the new list; the filter dialog remains open. If the name is missing/too short, the entry remains. If rejected or if the response is unclear, first check the list inventory, do not save repeatedly. Agency list and blacklist are not available to customer users here.

### Hit email and troubleshooting

The envelope in the filter sidebar opens **Subject** and **Description**. This is the separate sending path described under [Email directly from the filter hit list](./05b_employee_lists_filters.mdx#email-directly-from-the-filter-result-list): **Send email** requests sending immediately to the people selected in the filter, without a second confirmation and regardless of a side-only name search. No attachments, SMS, or message templates; the x closes without sending. The dialog closes immediately after the click, without confirming success or delivery. If the response is unclear, do not send again. For a more controllable process, first check the people overview with its selection and use the message dialog described above.

If no hits appear, first let it load, then check customer lists, date limits, repeated criteria, and both selection states. If lists, document types, or templates are missing, their loading may also have failed. Filter calculation and template search do not reliably end a loading indicator in every case when there is a network error. If loading persists, reopen the view; do not use any save or send action as a repair. If a request is rejected, have the specific access or save permission checked. A hit is neither a shift confirmation nor proof of valid documents.

### Table options in Employees and My lists

Both tables start with 20 rows per page and first name ascending. A separate selector for page size is not embedded here; page changes and sortable headings reload server-side. The entries/pages shown are not the full filter selection. The specific columns and the differing header-checkbox behavior are explained above under **Search, sort, and check selection of people**. In these two tables there is no column configurator, no directly editable cells, rating stars, or time approvals. The additional Excel field checkboxes change only the export, not the table columns.

The image and normal data cells lead to the Self-Service profile; a row itself does not trigger a bulk action. The people overview has no row actions. **My lists** shows the remove button; the limitation described above remains unchanged: the active button passes the wrong event format and does not trigger removal. Even without a selected list, it is not a pool-delete action. Do not click repeatedly; use the controlled profile path described earlier. Profile editing and assignment/time approvals are separate processes, not table functions enabled here.

## People profile in Self Service: display and independent actions

Open a person from **Employees** or **My lists**. First check the name at the top: both entry points lead to the people profile, not to the customer master data profile. The header shows, among other things, place of residence, age, email, and mobile number. A missing or rejected view is not an empty profile; do not try any changes as a repair. To check again, return to the people overview and reopen the person there. Simply changing the address in the browser does not replace this entry point.

### Distinguish display, editing, and saving

The profile is **not completely nonfunctional or read-only**. The normal customer-pool read view locks many master data fields; additionally assigned profile rights may differ from this. Customer lists under **Customer Pool** remain a separate, directly saving process as described above under **People profile: customer lists and automatic save actions**. **Reset password** is also a separate action. A greyed-out field proves neither lack of keyboard operation nor lack of server rights. If a supposedly locked field nevertheless accepts changes, do not keep editing; instead, ask administration for clarification.

Under **person details**, first name, last name, nickname, gender, number of children, smoking status, as well as date of birth and age can be read. First/last name and nickname have a 24-character limit in the form; first/last name are required fields with at least two characters. If profile editing is not available, the name and number-of-children fields are in fact read-only; the smoking switches are locked for customer users. Age is a displayed value, not a separately stored input value. A required-field marker is not an invitation for customers to add other people's personal details. Coordinate corrections to such master data with the responsible administration.

**No general cancel promise:** For fields whose editing is explicitly enabled, leaving a field can mark changes for saving. It searches the project name, stored client name, and location name, not the description or project number in general. Search text is applied immediately while typing, but is only stored later with actions such as filtering or changing tabs. Staged, not-yet-applied selections are separate from this.

**If the display is not correct:** first check the visible selection, search text, and tab. Do not filter repeatedly in quick succession: the list query cancels its previous request, but the additional filter and count queries are not jointly protected. Delayed responses can affect counters or intermediate data. If loading fails, reopen the view, check the restored filters, and tell administration the last action and the desired period.

**Project description** is optional. The text editor limits the entered text to **500 characters**; if this is exceeded, the edit path reverts to the last accepted content for this language, possibly to empty. Therefore shorten long texts before pasting and then check the visible content. Changing languages is not a translation. The separate translation action and detailed link editing are not part of this section.

**Check multilingual values after saving:** For the main name and main description, the save process first takes the language with an existing name in the order German, English, French, Italian. For customer users, the server also writes these main values into the language stored in the user account. This can differ from the tab you currently have open. As a result, an earlier different translation there may be replaced. After saving, check all required language tabs again; do not assume that every language version remains unchanged.

When leaving the name or description field in a **new project not yet created on the server**, a local save is attempted. This is not the saving of an existing server project. The general save limits below also apply here.

## Choose or add project type and customer project manager

Select a **project type** and a **project manager**. Both are mandatory. For existing entries, click the matching result and then click another field; Enter and Tab are not consistently pure selection keys here. In customer self-service, the project manager is a contact person of your customer, not an agency contact. Without customer context, these two selection lists cannot be selected normally. Selecting an existing entry first changes the assignment in the form; for a new item, the local draft save is triggered.

**New project type:** First search for an existing entry. If a genuinely new type is needed, type the full name again, check that no existing entry matches, and press **Tab** once. Enter is also bound as a creation trigger, but when a result is marked it can conflict with the selection; do not use it to confirm existing types. Warning: this requests creation of the type immediately, **without a separate type dialog and without waiting for any later continuation**. An exactly matching name in the loaded list is not created again; the server additionally checks whether the name is already in use in the respective customer or agency area. If successful, the new type is selected. If there is an error or no response, first check the list and permission, and do not press Enter repeatedly. The right to create a project does not automatically include creating a new type.

**New project lead via contact details:**

1. Only for a truly new contact: Type the name again in the **Project Manager** field and press **Tab** once. The **CONTACT DETAILS** dialog opens. Enter is also a dialog trigger, even if an existing result is selected at the same time. An unexpectedly opened dialog is not an instruction to save the existing contact again. If there are exactly two words separated by a space, the entered name is automatically split into first and last name; check the split and all prefilled fields.
2. Fill in **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **email**. Both names have a maximum of 24 characters. The email must match the form format; this does not check whether the address can actually be reached. **Mobile no.** is optional and limited to 16 characters.
3. Click **Save** once and wait. The contact is saved independently under the customer, then added to the list and selected as project manager. The project itself is not created yet. In this process, creating the contact does not send a user invitation and does not grant the contact person portal access.
4. **Cancel** or the close icon before submitting closes the dialog and discards its input. After submission has already been sent, closing is not a cancellation of the ongoing request; a late response can still create and select the contact. A later back action from the project does not delete this contact.

If there is an error, the contact creation remains unconfirmed. Before trying again, check whether the contact already exists. The Save button shows a loading state, but here it does not reliably prevent a second click. Edit rights for your own self-service projects alone may be insufficient for this separate contact creation; administration should check the specific process. You do not edit an already selected contact here via the hidden agency customer contact picker.

## Assign numbers, cost center, and department correctly

**Project no.** and **Order number** are optional internal details with up to 99 characters each. The project number can be checked against existing numbers. In the notice about an existing project number, **No** means: clear the number in the form. **Yes** keeps it; the close icon only closes the notice. None of these actions saves the entire project. The check runs asynchronously and does not reliably hold further actions until its response. Therefore do not rely on guaranteed uniqueness or on the notice preventing every duplicate creation.

**Cost center:** Type a value or select a suggestion. The suggestions are filtered by your text. Closing the list does not save an existing server project. If **Multiple cost centers in use.** is shown, the shared input field is replaced by this notice; check the differing values in role planning. During later overall saving, roles without a cost center can be carried along; if all existing non-empty role values correspond to the old project cost center, they can also switch to the new project value. This is not merely a display label.

**Department:** Select one of the departments loaded for your customer user. The normal list is based on your department assignments; an empty list can also result from a loading error. An offered entry **no department** if present is not the same as a normal department and is not reliably treated as an empty assignment in the immediate change path. If such an error message appears, involve administration. When **multiple departments in use** is shown, the shared picker is replaced by the notice; the roles must be checked separately.

- **New, not yet saved projects:** The department selection initially belongs to the form or local draft.
- **Already saved projects:** Selecting or clearing the picker sends a separate change request **immediately**, without Continue and without additional confirmation. In doing so, roles with the previous non-empty project department can also be changed. This is separate from overall saving, which can additionally take over roles without a department. Other deliberately deviating role departments are not automatically treated as identical.
- **New department:** Only if an additional department is truly needed, enter the full name again and press **Enter** once. Enter requests a customer department immediately, even if a result is already marked; therefore do not use it to select an existing department. This is neither an informal search nor a separate confirmation dialog. Afterwards, the new department is selected and, for existing projects, its change is also requested. The new creation can additionally extend the department assignments of customer users who were previously assigned to all existing departments; so it affects more than this project. The selection list returned after this creation can contain more departments than before; this does not automatically create additional edit rights.

Self-service edit rights alone do not guarantee that the separate department change will be accepted: this path uses a different rights check. If there is an error, the local value may still appear changed. Do not toggle repeatedly and quickly; responses and write requests here are not jointly protected against overtaking. After completion, check the saved project and role state by reopening it. **Back is not an undo for these immediate changes either.**

## Distinguish Continue, local save, and Back

Before **Continue**, check at least one valid project name, **project type**, the specified customer, and the **customer project manager**. In addition, at least one location must exist and **every added location must have assignment days**. For a project with a date range instead of individual days, both range boundaries must be present. If employee cancellations are enabled, the basic-data check requires a deadline of at least two days. Empty location rows can prevent Continue, even if another location is complete.

**Templates and drafts:** When using a project template, among other things the name, contact, type, cost center, and department are copied; the normal assignment-day lists are cleared. Select the required days again and check all transferred values. A locally reopened draft, on the other hand, is not a fresh template. The full template transfer as well as location, calendar, and recurrence handling are not declared complete here.

| Action | Storage limit |
| --- | --- |
| Leave a field or change a selection | AutoSave only saves a new item that has not yet been created on the server, and only in the same browser, provided no submit is currently running. Not every field has its own such trigger. An existing project is not saved on the server through this general AutoSave path. |
| **Continue** | Validates the form and submits the entire project state. After reported success, the matching local draft is removed and step 2, the Self Service project planning, is opened. This is not a publication, invitation, or confirmed staffing assignment. |
| **Back** | Takes you to the project overview, not to a discard confirmation. When leaving, a local save of the new item may still be initiated. Changes to an existing project are not generally saved this way; already separately saved master data or departments are not rolled back either. |
| Step number **2**, **3**, or **4** for a saved project | The transition is started before the subsequent save attempt. A follow-up page that opens on this path therefore does not prove a successful save. Step 3 additionally depends on the edit flag. Use Continue for a controlled finish and check its result. |

For a customer user in Self Service, the agency warning that another customer can no longer be selected later does not apply. If newly added past days are included, a separate yes/no prompt follows instead: **No** ends this save attempt, **Yes** continues it. Past days that were already saved when loading alone do not trigger this second warning. Rejecting it does not delete any contacts, types, or departments created independently beforehand.

**No shared undo boundary:** A local draft is neither a server-side project nor a permanent or cross-device backup. It may still exist after switching accounts in the same browser; check the context. The individual master-data requests, department change, and the overall save do not form a guaranteed atomic operation. During the overall save, project/role changes may already have happened before a later step fails. An error message therefore does not prove that nothing was saved.

If a save error occurs, first stay on the error message and check the current state in the overview before creating the item again. Tell administration the project name, customer account, action, and error message. If edit permission is missing, Continue can only lead to planning without saving. Simultaneous editing in multiple windows as well as late loading or write responses can lead to outdated values; a dirty warning or automatic conflict resolution is not guaranteed here.

## Treat the image and subsequent settings separately

An image upload is not proof that the project has already been saved. If your account is allowed to perform the upload, an unsaved item is first given a file with a temporary record; only the later project save path transfers the image reference. For an existing project, a successful upload can change the image reference immediately. This upload check is not the same as the Self Service creation permission. A preview image or a visual lock alone does not confirm successful saving or a complete access lock.

The exact upload/delete controls, visibility switches, automatic invite/booking/cancellation functions, time-tracking managers including the invitation dialog, project deletion, and cancellation are **not part of this limited basic-data guide**. Likewise, location/map/calendar and the subsequent roles, wages, expenses, and criteria remain separate steps. Do not use their visible actions as a substitute for Continue or for merely cancelling. A button on a subsequent page means neither that the basic data is saved nor that an order was published or a message delivered.

## Select locations in the project basic data

As a customer user, open **Self Service → Project overview → Add new project** or an existing project via its name. The following steps supplement the basic-data guide above; they are not instructions for creating an order. Wait for the project data to load. A template can bring locations with it, even though its normal assignment days were cleared.

1. Search in the location field for the saved name, a Google place name, or coordinates. If the list is empty, **Select location** opens the selection. Pressing Enter on an empty field does not create anything.
2. Select an existing database match. It is added to the form, not created again as a master record. The newly added location is marked for calendar editing, and the previously marked locations are deselected. An identical name already present in the form is not added again.
3. Check the address beneath the name; **No address** is not a geocoding confirmation. The name field in the project row is read-only. Expanding shows the individual days, and the number beside it counts the days in that row.
4. Repeat the selection for additional locations. Tick exactly the locations on the left whose days you want to edit together. Opening a row alone does not change this selection; clicking its map pin, however, can additionally select the location.

Search and map load asynchronously. An older search result may arrive after a newer input; compare the selected name and address before continuing. A missing map or missing results do not prove that there is no location. A location added without days can later block Continue. Selecting an existing location is not yet a server-side save of the project assignment.

## Create a new location — do not rename an existing one

**Create a free name:** Type a new name into the location field and press Enter. If Enter has just selected a marked result, that result is applied first; the goal is not to open a second dialog at the same time. The location dialog shows **Database name** as a required field and a read-only second field with the Google place name. No German translation is stored for its key, so no fixed German label is assumed here. An empty Google entry is not a required-field error.

**Google result:** A location that has not yet been saved is usually shown on the map first. Check the point and click **Create location** in the map window. Only then does the name dialog open. If the map view is not yet available, the dialog can appear immediately. If location resolution fails, a preview may be missing; it is not a saved location.

**Coordinates:** Enter `47.37688, 8.54169`, for example. Check the preview and open the same dialog from there. For a coordinate point, the database name must first be entered manually. Even without a found address, the coordinate path can open the dialog. By contrast, a freely clicked map point without a resolvable address is cancelled with a notice. The selected coordinates are not intentionally replaced by the center of the found street address.

Click **Save** only once and wait: **this immediately requests a separate location master record, before Continue on the project.** The customer user creates it for their own customer. If successful, the location is added to the form and the dialog closes. An already existing name in the same area results in a notice instead of a second creation. The project assignment and days still require saving the project.

**Cancel** or the close icon before submitting closes the dialog and removes the provisional map point; it is not a delete request. After submission, closing does not stop the request. A delayed response can close a dialog that has since been reopened or add a location later. The loading indicator does not prevent a second submission here. In case of transport errors it may remain stuck; in the event of a business error response, the dialog still closes. First check the location list instead of blindly creating it again. Even a success message phrased awkwardly does not replace this check.

**Edit an existing name or address:** Not in the read-only project row and not by creating the same name again. The separate [location management](./07b_locations) has its own edit path and different save consequences. A general master-data edit dialog is not mounted here as a project action; **Change location** means something else, see below.

## Use the map and add missing coordinates

A pin for an already assigned location opens or marks its project row. Pale available locations can be added by clicking them; they appear only in the matching map section and with sufficient zoom. Their availability depends on the loaded location list, not on a guaranteed global search. A map display alone does not save either days or the project.

Under **Locations not shown on the map**, you can find locations without usable coordinates. If **Place on map** is offered:

1. Click the action for the correct location. An automatically suggested point is only a suggestion.
2. Click the desired map point and move the provisional pin if needed. Check the name in the map window.
3. Click **Save location** and read the confirmation: the coordinates are saved on the existing location and can make the distance warning when clocking in effective even for existing projects with this location. This is **not merely a local project draft** and not proven GPS/warning delivery.
4. Only confirmation sends the coordinate change. No does not send it; **Cancel** ends placement without a new write. After submission, undo is not guaranteed here either.

This path adds missing coordinates; it is not a general move mode for locations that are already fully geolocated. If coordinates appear in the meantime, the server may report a conflict instead of a change; the view then ends placement mode without taking over the success. Recheck in case of errors or parallel editing. While a confirmation is open, do not change the location, cancel, or move the pin again: the confirmation is not fully protected against such local changes. Map/geocoding services may fail. Archive/maintenance status blocks individual actions; a visual form overlay alone is not a reliable permission check.

## Edit individual days, multiple selection, and date ranges

For normal projects, open **Date range**. At least one location must be ticked on the left, otherwise the calendar is not active. With multiple marked locations, it shows their common set of days — a mark does not mean that this day already exists at every location.

- **Add day:** Click a calendar day that is not yet marked. It is added to the selected locations. Check the expanded day lists per location.
- **Add consecutive days:** Drag in the calendar from the first to the last day. Both boundaries are included, even when dragging in reverse. Existing days are retained; dragging is not a replacement and not a removal.
- **Remove a day from the calendar selection:** Click a marked day again. The selected locations are affected, not automatically all project locations.
- **Remove a day from only one row:** Expand that location row and click the remove icon next to the date. This is not removing the entire location.

For a day that has already been saved, a request is sent to check existing employee assignments before local removal. **This check does not yet delete the day on the server.** If rejected or if an error occurs, the day remains or is restored in the calendar. Only Continue transmits the changed day lists; assigned employees are checked again at that point. A locally disappeared day is therefore not final proof of deletion. After removing individual days, do not reorder/remove rows or start additional deletions at the same time: delayed responses can act on list positions that have already moved.

**Date-range projects:** If the loaded project is managed as a date-range/pay-per-call project, a from-to calendar is active instead of multiple selection; this basic-data view does not offer a separate switch for this project type. Select a location, then start and end. Only one location is selected at a time for the date-range change. The range is applied only when both boundaries are set; clicking only a new start can still leave the previous complete range in place. Therefore check the displayed boundaries of each row. The recurrence tabs are not offered in this mode.

**Continue checks all rows**, including those not ticked: normal locations need at least one day, date-range locations need both boundaries. The check does not verify every conceivable date order. Past newly added days can first trigger a notice and then, when saving, a separate yes/no prompt; No ends this save attempt without rolling back earlier master-data writes. Check the calendar and day list again after a failed save attempt. Browser time zones and the conversion of dates already present as text may differ; there is no guarantee here of a time-zone-independent transfer. A local draft is not a server-side date backup.

## Generate recurring assignment dates and remove exceptions

For a normal project, first tick the desired locations and open **Recurring pattern**. Without a selected location, or when archive/maintenance status applies, the pattern cannot be applied. This is a helper for generating concrete assignment days, **not an indefinitely continuing series** and not automatic staffing planning.

1. Set **From** and **To**. When opened for the first time, From is prefilled with today’s local date; To remains to be completed. An empty, invalid, or reversed range does not generate preview days. Both valid boundaries are included.
2. Choose **Weekly** and under **Repeat on** select the desired weekdays. You can use every week, every second, third, or fourth week. Counting starts with the Monday-to-Sunday week in which From falls — not only with the first selected weekday.
3. Alternatively, choose **Monthly**, then the first, second, third, fourth, or last desired weekday of the month. “Last” is not automatically “fourth.” There is no selection for a fifth occurrence and no separate public-holiday/vacation calendar here.
4. Check **Preview**, day count, and date list. For longer results, only the first seven days and the number of additional days are shown initially. Changes to the pattern fields only recalculate the preview.
5. Click **Apply pattern**. You return to the date range. The concrete days are added to the currently selected locations; already existing days and their assignments are retained. Check each location, especially if multiple different day lists were marked. The message count is not the sum of all newly created location-day combinations.

**Exceptions:** Remove a single day via its location list or a targeted calendar selection. Applying the same pattern again can add this day back: no permanent series exception is saved. Changing the preview does not remove previously applied days either.

**Remove matching days** removes only the days matching the current pattern from the currently selected locations. Unsaved days are removed locally right away; saved days are checked for assignments first. Days that cannot be removed remain. The summary warning refers to employee assignments, but it can also aggregate failed check requests this way. Do not change the selection or days any further in the meantime: the response uses the location selection current at that point. Only Continue saves the resulting lists. Apply pattern and Remove matching days are not a publication, not a recurring series rule, and not a shared undo boundary.

## Change location, remove location, and check save consequences

**Change Location moves project days — it does not edit the master-data address.** For normal projects with more than one location, click **Change Location** on the source row, then **Add here** at the destination. Before the second click, you can choose **Cancel** on the source row without moving any days. When applied, the days are merged at the destination, duplicate days are avoided, and the source row is removed from the form. There is no additional yes/no prompt and initially no server write.

**Caution with existing projects:** If you then continue, this change can move existing project appointments and roles to the target location. For affected current/future confirmed or self-booked assignments, the status can be reset to assigned and a change notification triggered. This is neither a guaranteed delivery nor a new personal acceptance. Afterwards, check roles, shifts, and assignments; simply moving the day list is not a complete planning check. Do not save carelessly when locations have the same name or have been corrected in the meantime: parts of the save path resolve locations by name, not solely by the visible ID.

**Remove the entire location from the project:** Click the remove icon on the location row and confirm the prompt. No does not remove anything. If the row was added only locally, Yes removes it only from the form. If a project-location assignment has already been saved, however, Yes sends an **immediate separate deletion request**, without waiting for Continue. Existing employee assignments or missing permission can block it; the separate deletion path does not simply require the same Self-Service permission as Continue. If successful, associated roles, shifts, staffing requirements, and other planning data can also be removed. This is not a deletion of the general location master record. Do not remove multiple rows in parallel: a late response uses the earlier list position.

| Action | What is saved? |
| --- | --- |
| Select an existing location, change days/period, apply pattern | First form state; Continue is required for the project assignment or day lists. Not every one of these actions triggers its own local save. |
| New location: save in the name dialog | Separate master data record created immediately; returning to the project does not delete it. |
| Missing coordinates: save location and confirm | Separate change to the saved location immediately, not only after Continue. |
| Remove a single saved day | First assignment check and local change, only Continue saves the day list. |
| Saved location row: confirm removal | Separate immediate project-location deletion path, not merely local removal. |
| Change location → Add here | Local merge plus noted change; effect on roles/appointments when the project is saved later. |

After Continue, reopen the locations and date lists to check them. An error message does not mean that all earlier partial changes were rolled back; a successful assignment check does not reserve the state against other editors. If anything is unclear, provide the administration with the project, location name, specific date, action, and message instead of blindly creating or deleting again. The subsequent role/shift planning, wages, expenses, criteria, automation, publication, and overall project deletion remain separate processes that are not fully checked here.

## Saving a project template: the correct starting point

In the **project master data (step 1)** there is currently **no active entry point for saving a template**. Do not look there for a template name dialog. **Load from template** in the project overview is a different process: it opens a new project from an existing project template; it does not update that template.

For the available save path:

1. Check the master data and save it with **Continue**. Wait for the confirmed result; merely opening the next page does not prove that all changes have been saved.
2. Wait in **Project planning (step 2)** until the correct project has been loaded with its customer and ongoing changes are finished. Check the required roles and shifts. The following instructions cover only the project template action, not the entire planning process.
3. Click **Save as project template** above the planning. The dialog is called **Save as template** and contains **Template name**.

The visual lock on the entry point depends on whether the project can be edited; it is not proof of template write permission. Server-side saving or overwriting requires the permission to edit templates or the permission to edit all Self Service projects. Having only the permission to edit your own Self Service projects is not automatically sufficient for this template path. If you lack permission, involve the administration; do not use another customer account.

## Naming and saving a new project template

**Before saving:** A project template is a separate saved record for later reuse. It is neither the local draft save nor a substitute for saving your project changes. In particular, check the project, customer account, and desired template name.

1. Enter a clear, non-empty name in **Template name**. This is the template name, not renaming the project. An empty field leads to **Name is required**; do not use only spaces either.
2. In this dialog there is **no type selection, no customer picker, no visibility switch, and no list of existing templates**. The type is project template; in Self Service the customer area is passed through from the loaded project and bound server-side to the permitted customer context. The visibility of the customer name for employees is something different from this template scope.
3. Click **Save** once. Pressing Enter in the name field triggers the same save attempt. The dialog closes immediately, before the response arrives — even with an empty name. Closing alone is therefore not proof of success.
4. If the name is new, a new project template is requested. Wait for the message and then check via **Load from template** in the Self-Service project overview whether the desired name is offered. Opening the list alone does not create a project; only an intentional selection starts creation from the template.

The name check takes into account the template type, agency, and customer area. The same name in another template family is not automatically the same template. A new name does not create a new version with a guaranteed history; use a distinct name for a deliberately separate template.

## Overwrite an existing project template or choose No

If a project template with this name already exists in the same area, the confirmation **A project template with the name … already exists. Update this template?** appears. There is no additional selection of another template here. The entered name determines the found entry; the template loaded earlier when creating it is not automatically overwritten just because of that origin.

**Caution before Yes:** You replace the content of the found template for later use. Check the name and project once again. The new content is assembled only when you confirm, from the then current project state; therefore, while the confirmation is open, do not switch projects or continue editing.

- **No** does not request an overwrite. The previous name check has therefore not become a new template record. Open the name dialog again if needed and use a different name.
- **Yes** sends a separate update of the found project template. Wait for **Project template updated successfully.** and check the desired content the next time it is used. A message alone does not replace content verification in the event of errors or parallel editing.
- The checked project template update path replaces the template content, **not automatically the projects already created from it**. These are not treated as continuously synchronized copies. Already open forms can still contain the old state; reopening later can load the new state.

The confirmation offers no versioning or undo function. If an update was made by mistake, do not save blindly again: clarify the template name, customer, and desired state with the administration.

## What is copied when loading a project template

After **Load from template** in the project overview, a new project is opened. Check the values before **Continue** instead of expecting a complete, unchanged copy:

| Area | Behavior in the master data |
| --- | --- |
| Name and description | Main values and existing language versions are copied. |
| Customer, project management, and project type | Saved assignments are copied; the selection lists are reloaded for the template customer. Do not proceed if the customer context is not appropriate. |
| Numbers, cost center, and department | Project number, order number, cost center, and department assignment are copied. A copied project number may already be in use. |
| Locations and assignment days | Location objects remain, but **normal assignment day lists and their saved day identifiers are cleared**. The days must be selected again. If the location list is missing in an older template, this part starts empty. This does not guarantee that every other location field or a period is also reset. |
| Other saved master data | Among other things, image reference, parent project, status/archive flags, period type, buyer, timekeeping managers, and automation/employee visibility values can be copied. Some fields are not directly visible in the customer form. Check unusual states before saving; the respective settings control is not enabled here. |

For older templates without the relevant visibility values, the customer is visible to employees by default, but the project management and timekeeping managers are not. Missing automatic invitation is copied as switched off. A copied setting alone is not an invitation, booking, publication, or confirmed personnel assignment.

The new project identifier is not copied from the template in this master-data loading path. **Continue** saves the new project. When subsequently moving to planning, the template can be loaded again and embedded roles can be created separately; this is not just displaying again and is not a guaranteed atomic overall process. Therefore, do not change or overwrite the template in parallel with an ongoing creation. Full role/shift transfer and personnel planning remain separate processes.

Wait for loading to finish before changing values: a delayed template response can replace entries you have entered in the meantime. If a template is unreadable or language data is faulty, do not select it repeatedly; provide the template and the message to the administration.

## Distinguish Cancel, errors, and independent saving

**Cancel** or the close icon in the name dialog before sending closes it without a template request. An already submitted request is not withdrawn by doing this. The name is not explicitly cleared when closing; check it the next time you open it.

| Action | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Local AutoSave in the master data | Saves only a new record not yet created on the server in the same browser; does not update a project template. |
| Continue in the master data | Saves the project, not automatically its source template. |
| Save or Enter in the project template name dialog | Separate template request; no shared save process for all open project changes. |
| Yes when overwriting | Separate update of the template record. |
| Back, Cancel, or No | Does not undo a previously successful independent save. |

When creating the template content, project details are merged with saved roles and shifts. Missing certain master data is supplemented from the saved project; if the location list is missing or empty, it can also be reloaded. Therefore, first finish the desired project changes: a preview or still-open role editing does not guarantee that exactly this visible intermediate state will end up in the template.

If there is an **error**, no message, or an unexpected confirmation, first check the existing template state. The dialog does not have reliable double-click protection. Transport errors are not separately caught in the save handler; a missing response proves neither success nor a safe non-write state. A delayed success message can close a name dialog that has been reopened in the meantime; a delayed name-conflict response can open a confirmation only after that dialog has been closed. Do not press Enter or Save repeatedly and do not choose Yes too quickly. Give the administration the project, customer account, template name, last action, and message.

## Do not confuse project templates with role templates

**Save as project template** applies to the project together with embedded role information. A separate **role template** is a different template family: when saving a role template separately, cost center and department references are removed from the template content. The project template uses a separate structure: its embedded roles contain cost center references. The transfer of the department in the project master data is separate from this; this does not mean that role-based department assignments are copied unchanged. Do not transfer the rules between the two families.

The name dialog described here changes neither the template type nor the customer assignment of an existing template. To create a new project, you choose a project template via the project overview; the separate role template picker and the template management have different processes. Successful template saving is not permission to perform all role, wage, expense, criteria, publication, or assignment actions.

Detailed template management remains separate. Likewise, this section does not cover inviting people responsible for time tracking, using the visibility/automation switches, media/translation editing, or deleting and cancelling entire projects. These actions are not a substitute for cancelling a template dialog.


## Select existing people responsible for time tracking

The following sections supplement the master-data instructions for **customer users in Self Service**. The field in question is **Person responsible for time tracking**, not the customer project manager and not the general employee invitation to register.

1. Open the new or existing Self-Service project in step 1 and wait for the loaded master data.
2. Click in **select or invite person(s) responsible for time tracking**. Search by name. The search filters the loaded names locally, without case sensitivity; it is not an email search and not a new server fetch.
3. **Click an existing result** to select it. Multiple people are possible; the list remains open after a selection. Remove an unneeded selection via its remove icon; this does not delete a user account and does not revoke an invitation already sent.
4. Check the selected names and save the project assignment with **Continue**. A selection alone does not send an invitation. In a newly created project that has not yet been saved, the selection can trigger local draft saving; in an existing project, it does not replace the later project save.

**Enter is not a safe confirmation of a result here:** In the open selection field, Enter opens the invitation dialog, even when the search text is empty or an existing result is highlighted. Therefore, click the result to select it. Close an accidentally opened dialog with **Cancel**, not with Save.

For normally loaded Self-Service data, the suggestions come from the user assignments belonging to the customer whose customer role has the permission for responsibility for time tracking. A matching additional agency/customer assignment of an existing account can also be considered. Not all employees or customer contacts are simply offered. After a new invitation, however, the list returned immediately can also contain agency users; the list restriction is not identical on these two response paths. If unexpected people appear, do not select blindly, but have the customer context checked and reopen the project.

An empty list can mean missing matching users, missing read permission, or a loading error. Archived or still pending order statuses block the picker; missing project edit permission and form overlay are separate from that. A visual block or an accessible dialog does not prove server-side permission. If the control cannot be used, involve administration; do not bypass the block.


## Open the invitation dialog for a new person and check the fields

First check whether the person is already selectable as an existing user. The dialog is **not a re-invitation of an existing account** and not merely the creation of a contact person.

1. Type the new name into the **Person responsible for time tracking** field and press **Enter**. The **Invite person responsible for time tracking** dialog opens; its role list is loaded separately. Opening it alone does not send anything yet.
2. Check **First Name** and **Last Name**. The search text is taken over in full as the first name and is not automatically split into two names. The last name must be filled in separately.
3. Enter the correct **email**. It is both the recipient address and the address of the new user account to be created. Check the spelling and recipient before sending; the form does not confirm deliverability.
4. Select **role** and **language**. The language list offers **English, German, French, and Italian**. The role comes from the time-tracking roles enabled for the agency, not from a fixed, always identical role list. Retrieving the role additionally requires the corresponding time-tracking permission of the acting user. Agency roles may also be offered; for a customer user, clarify the intended customer role with administration. Selecting a role is not merely a label for this project.
5. Before every submission, check **all five fields again**. On first opening, last name, email, role, and language are empty. When reopening, old entries and old role options may remain; only the first name is set again from the search text. A delayed role response can replace the list.

All five fields must be filled in. If values are missing, **Please fill in all mandatory fields before saving.** appears. The validation is not a reliable check for spaces only, a valid email address, or the current membership of the selected role in the loaded list. Do not use names consisting only of spaces. The dialog offers **no phone number, no department selection, and no editable message preview**. In particular, the department selection from the separate Client Buyer dialog must not be transferred to this dialog.


## Saving in the invitation dialog sends its own request immediately

**Warning: saving in this dialog does not wait for Continue in the project.** It immediately requests a new user account and an invitation. This works as a separate process even before the first project save. Submit only if you intend to create the account and send the invitation.

1. Check the email, names, role, and language; also check the project name that is copied into the invitation. If name changes have not yet been saved, this may differ from the language field currently visible.
2. Click **Save** once. There is **no additional Yes/No send confirmation**. The loading indicator is not a reliable block against a second click.
3. If the request is answered successfully, **Invitation sent successfully.** appears, the dialog closes, and the returned user ID is added to the local project selection.
4. Then check the selection and save the **project assignment separately with Continue**. The invitation request does not send any request to save this project assignment. Even the success response does not itself trigger the local draft save here.

The account creation saves a user account that is initially unconfirmed and an invitation entry. For customer roles, a downstream department assignment is also triggered. This is **not a single project save that can be undone**. A technical limitation can prevent the new account from being assigned to the customer; therefore, a success message does not prove that the person will be available in the correct customer picker after reloading or that they can already access it. If the person is missing, have the account, customer, role, and department assignment checked instead of creating a second account.

If the email is already assigned to a user account that has not been deleted, this creation path rejects it as already existing. It then sends no new invitation and does not automatically link the existing account to the project. This also applies if the person does not appear in your list. A different spelling or a new address is not a suitable way to bypass this conflict; have the existing access and assignment clarified.


## Distinguish cancel, errors, and actual sending

**Cancel** and the close icon at the top close the dialog. Before sending, this creates neither an account nor an invitation; however, old entries are not explicitly cleared. After clicking **Save**, closing the dialog is **not a cancellation of the request already in progress**. Going back from the project, removing the local selection, or discarding a local project draft also does not undo the standalone account creation or a send that has already taken place.

A late success response can still add the selection and even close a dialog that has since been reopened. While loading, do not change the recipient address and do not send again: the already submitted request keeps its old address. Multiple sends are not reliably prevented; the same ID can also be added locally multiple times by multiple responses. Simultaneous loading responses can temporarily replace the suggestion list.

In the event of a transport error, the loading indicator ends, but a response with a different business status may leave it running. An error message or missing response does not prove that no account or invitation entry has been saved yet. **Before trying again, have the existing user and invitation status checked.** Tell administration the project, intended recipient, action, and error message; do not forward access credentials.

The intended email route addresses the entered email and uses **you@job.rocks** as the sender together with the platform brand name. In German, the subject is **Einladung als Verantwortlicher Zeiterfassung**. The message contains the project name and temporary access credentials; it is not the general employee registration invitation and not an invitation message for an employee shift.

**Request accepted ≠ sent ≠ delivered ≠ login or invitation accepted.** The email route may fail to send, for example because of a blocked address, sending environment, or mail error, while account creation still returns a success response. The message **Invitation sent successfully.** is therefore not a reliable confirmation of sending or delivery. First check the account creation, then the sending status, and if necessary the spam folder with the intended person. A successfully created account alone confirms neither a login nor a completed project assignment.


## Separate visibility for employees and time-tracking permissions

Next to **Person responsible for time tracking** there is the **visible to employees** switch. It controls whether the selected people responsible for time tracking are passed on as contacts in the corresponding employee assignment data. **First name, last name, and email** may become visible; no phone number of the user account is delivered through this contact path. Activate it only if these contact details should be visible.

The switch first changes the project form and here has no separate immediate save or send action. Save with **Continue** and check the status after reopening. In a newly created empty project, this visibility is off; loaded templates or drafts can bring a different value. Switching it on or off does not invite anyone, does not change any user role, and is not the approval of working hours. Switching it off also does not remove the person from the responsible-person selection and does not revoke access.

The saved responsible-person selection is used for the associated project list for people responsible for time tracking; additional prerequisites apply there, such as matching shifts and time/assignment data that are still relevant. **An assignment therefore guarantees neither a visible task nor an unrestricted review or edit right.** Role permissions and the respective time actions are checked separately. Full time-tracking and expense validation, other visibility switches, project deletion, and automation functions are not covered by this guide.


## Visibility and automations: open and save deliberately

As a client user, open **Self Service → Projects**, select the relevant project, and open step **1** (basic data). For a new project, start via the project creation path already described. Wait until the project data has loaded. The automations are located below the fields for number, cost center, and department; the visibility switches are directly beside the respective responsible persons.

1. First check that you are editing the correct project and the correct template. The automations mentioned below are switched off in a fresh project without a template; the cancellation period is empty. A loaded template or an existing project can have different values.
2. Change the desired switches and, if necessary, the cancellation period. **Switching them initially only changes the form. It neither sends an invitation immediately nor triggers its own save request for the switch.** Unlike the flags in the project overview, this is not an immediately saved table action.
3. Also check the remaining basic data and click **next**. This saves the entire project form, not just the last changed switch. Wait for a successful response. Then reopen the basic data and check the values and affected shifts. For subsequently turning on **auto-close**, the special immediate consequence below applies.

The switches themselves do not trigger a local autosave action. In a new project, a later field action or leaving the view can locally save the current overall draft in the browser; when the cancellation period is exited, this path is explicitly called. That is not server-side saving. For existing projects, there is no local draft save beyond that. **Back is therefore neither save nor a reliable discard.** There is no shared reset button for these settings here. A rejection prompt before saving does not start this project save process, but it does not reset the switches either.

Do not change anything when archived, when the order status is pending, or when edit permission is missing. The main form is then visually overlaid; the individual switches are not all technically blocked in the same way. An input that still responds is not authorization. For a new project, the Self Service creation right applies; for existing projects, the appropriate all/own edit right together with responsibility and client/agency area applies. Visibility for employees does not grant any of these rights.

Do not perform any further switch tests while loading or saving. A delayed loading response can replace local changes; an old successful save response can be forwarded despite newer local changes. Error messages do not reliably reset the local switches to the saved state. If the response is unclear, reopen and check first, do not save blindly again. Switching off after a successful save is a new change and does not undo invitations, closures, or messages that have already been created.

**Templates and missing values:** Templates carry over self booking, cancellation, deadline, republishing, and auto-close. If one of these values is missing in an old template, it is not reliably supplemented by a new form default; check the display before saving. When saving, missing automation values are treated as off. If project manager visibility is missing in a template, it is treated as off; if the client name switch is missing, it is treated as visible. An explicitly saved off value is not the same as a missing value. For existing projects, the loaded project state is retained; the default values of a new project do not replace it. The separate visibility of the persons responsible for time tracking remains as described above.


## Project manager visible to employees: known limitation

Next to **Project Manager** there is **visible to employees**. First select the intended project manager, set the switch, and save with **next**. This switch does not grant portal rights, invite anyone, or automatically notify the project manager. It is also not the visibility switch for **Responsible for Time Tracking** explained above.

**Important in Self Service:** The project manager is selected here from the client contacts. However, in the tested state, the contact display for employees reads the agency project manager, not this selected client contact. Therefore, switching it on is not a reliable way to show the selected Self Service project manager in the employee app. If an agency contact has been adopted, that one may appear instead. Check the contact that is actually displayed and have administration clarify the assignment if it differs. Do not rely on this switch alone for required assignment information.

The separate switch for the client name is hidden in this client view. Agency users may have a different view on the same Self Service path; this does not create any additional client-side operation path. For new projects, the client name is generally preselected as visible. A template or a saved project can hide it. This is separate from contact display, the client's access to the project, and time tracking rights. **Invite Client Buyer** is also not a dialog reachable here through this visibility switch.


## Automatic invitation upon activation

Activate **auto-invite upon activation** and save with **next** if newly activated employees should be considered for suitable assignments in the future. This refers to activating an employee assignment to the agency, not opening, saving, or activating this project. Creating an already active employee assignment for the first time can also trigger the background process. Switching it on alone does not retroactively invite all already active people.

At processing time, the background process considers projects of the same agency with the status open or ongoing and the flag switched on. For a role, there must be a saved employee filter and open requirements from today's date onward with a positive target quantity including overbooking. Invitations are only created if the activated person matches the role filter. Existing entries for the same person and the same requirement are not created again, even if their status is no longer open. This is not a promise for every employee in the client pool or for every project.

**Distinguish notification and acceptance:** This activation path creates pending invitations and a related automatic publication entry; it does not include any separate email or push delivery to the employee. An invitation or history entry is therefore not proof of sending or delivery. The person is also not yet assigned and has not accepted. Check the actual invitations and communicate required assignment information through the intended channel. Background queue, processing, and visible final state are separate; if there are delays, do not try to help by repeatedly deactivating and reactivating.

Switching it off and saving does not prevent already created invitations from remaining in place. A background process already running can continue processing the state it read earlier; there is no cancel button for this run.


## Self booking instead of mere application

Activate **self booking** in the basic data and save with **next** if employees should be able to book an offered assignment themselves. Switching it on alone does not accept an existing application and does not automatically assign anyone. Only the corresponding action by the employee on an invitation starts the self-booking path; without self booking, the application path remains separate.

A self booking first sets the relevant invitations to accepted and then tries to assign them into free slots. For this, it searches today's and future requirements with a free assignment slot, a matching accepted application, and no detected shift overlap. It can still fail to create a slot, for example if none is free anymore by then. The checked assignment run validates the project and can also take further matching accepted invitations of the same person in this project into account; do not infer the scope solely from the last clicked offer. **Check the actual shift assignments**, not just an accepted invitation or success message.

The notification email for self booking can be triggered before this assignment attempt. Recipients are the non-empty client list for this event, otherwise the agency list; without either, there are no recipients for it. Such an email proves neither a filled slot nor delivery. If a self booking actually occurs, membership in the employee pool of the project agency is also ensured; any problem there is handled separately. Switching it off does not retract existing self bookings or pool memberships. Self booking does not replace any general access, assignment, or time tracking rights.


## Auto-close: existing shifts can be affected immediately

**Before switching it on for an existing project, check the open shifts and applications.** Set **auto-close** and save with **next**. When switching from off to on, already sufficiently staffed open shifts in the project are checked and closed during this save operation. This is not just a rule for later shifts; existing data can also be affected. There is no separate closing confirmation for this. When creating a new project or saving again with an already enabled flag, this special retrospective check is not started again in the same way.

Confirmed assignments and self-bookings count as staffed, not merely assigned but unconfirmed places and not just accepted applications. If this number reaches the staffing requirement, the closing path can take effect. The overbooking amount is not added to the number required for this; staffing requirements without a positive total are not closed in the closing path. Later corresponding assignment/confirmation actions can also trigger the check.

Closing is more than a flag: pending and automatically accepted invitations are removed in the closing path. Accepted applications are closed; for applicants without an already assigned or confirmed shift of the same role, a push notification **shift filled** can be triggered. Self-bookings are not treated the same for this notification exception: a person who booked themselves can also receive such a message. Existing assignments are not generally cancelled by this; if a notification conflicts, check the actual shift status. A suppressed or failed push notification is not proof that closing did not happen.

Turning it off and saving does not automatically reopen already closed shifts or restore removed invitations. If an assignment is later removed, a closed shift can fall below the staffing requirement and be reopened while the flag remains switched on. Reopening alone is not a new invitation and not a delivery confirmation; the separate republishing is relevant for that.


## Auto-republish when an assignment becomes available again

Set **auto-republish when an assignment becomes available again** and save with **next**. This is a rule for later supported cancellation/release processes, not an immediate publish button. Not every decrease in staffing triggers the same path: employee cancellation uses it directly; the general removal path additionally requires a recorded cancellation and a previously confirmed assignment or self-booking.

The affected shift must exist, be open, and not be before today's date. Its role needs a saved filter with suitable people. A shift that is still closed is skipped; the automatic reopening explained above can therefore be crucial for the interaction. The cancelling employee is omitted from this repeated invitation.

**Warning: In the tested version, automatic republishing can clear the stored application deadlines for all roles in this project — not just the deadline for the shift that has become free again.** This happens before the later selection of individual recipients and can therefore also occur if all initially found people are excluded afterwards. After such a process, check the application deadlines for all roles in the project with the responsible planner. Turning it off does not restore application deadlines that have already been cleared. The cancellation deadline in days is separate from this.

For suitable active people, the process tries to create missing invitations. If the staffing requirement is 0, the invitation helper can skip the new creation; a push notification alone therefore does not prove that an invitation was created here either. Check the actual staffing quantities and invitation list. Already pending invitations can receive a push notification again; other existing response statuses are not simply reset. The automatic publication entry and notification entries are not delivery confirmation. Repeated similar notifications are suppressed within a short time window; this does not guarantee one message per shift nor does it reliably protect against any parallel duplicate processing. This republishing path uses push jobs, not a separate email broadcast to employees.

Turning it off does not reverse invitations, publication entries, or requested push notifications that have already been created. If nothing new appears after a cancellation, check the saved switch, open status, date, role filter, and existing invitation statuses. Do not test by cancelling again and do not start repeated publication as a repair measure.


## Employee cancellation and setting the cancellation deadline

Activate **employee cancellation**. This makes the field **cancellation deadline (days)** appear. Enter at least **2** days, leave the field, and save with **next**. Blank, 0, negative values, or values below 2 prevent the form from being released when cancellation is enabled. Use whole days; the form does not contain reliable additional integer/max-value validation. When turning it off, the deadline field is hidden, but its previous value is not deleted. Therefore check the deadline again when turning it back on.

The employee app compares the distance to the start of each assigned shift in minutes with the configured number of days times 24 hours. At the exact boundary, the cancellation action is still visible. This is not about the end date of the overall project and not just about calendar days. This display check is not a guarantee of a comprehensive server-side deadline lock. If there are deviations or the action is unavailable, contact the responsible planner; do not try other ways to bypass it.

The switch itself does not cancel an assignment and does not send a message. Only a cancellation carried out and confirmed by the employee can remove the assignment, trigger the reopening/republishing rules, and initiate a cancellation email to configured recipients. Here too, a non-empty customer recipient list is used before the agency list. An email can be blocked or fail without this implying a reversal of the cancellation. Check the assignment status separately. Changing deadlines and turning the feature off do not undo cancellations that have already been processed.

**Do not confuse this:** In this customer master data form, there is no additional accessible NOH or general "auto-accept" switch. The rules for reorders, publication, and time tracking are separate processes. Do not use self-booking or the cancellation deadline as a substitute for this.

As a customer user, you will not find an accessible **Invite Client Buyer** dialog in these self-service master data: the associated selection and opening area is hidden for customer users. The separate dialog **Select or invite a time-tracking supervisor** is a different process. Do not transfer its instructions or role selection to the Client Buyer.

An agency user can open the same self-service address with a different view. That does not make their buyer invitation a customer function. If responsibility is wrong, have the stored buyer assignment checked by the responsible administration; merely seeing a dialog name in another view is not proof of your authorization.


## Distinguish Back, confirmation, and destructive actions

**Check before confirming:** **Back**, **delete project**, and **cancel project** are three different processes. Deletion and cancellation are requested immediately after **Yes**; **next** is not required for that. There is no guaranteed reversal, refund, or shared all-or-nothing processing here.

- **Back** returns to the self-service project overview without a deletion or cancellation request. When leaving, an unsaved project draft can be automatically saved locally. Back is therefore not a safe discard.
- **delete project** and **cancel project** appear when a project name exists and the permission **delete & cancel self service project** is present. The normal agency right to cancel a job does not replace this self-service right. Archived projects and pending or rejected drafts are additionally overlaid in this view. A greyed-out area is not a promise about all technical blocks.
- For a saved self-service process, the special delete/cancel right and the appropriate customer/agency area are checked again. This is not automatically limited to projects where you yourself are the buyer; view or edit own/all projects are different permissions.
- In the confirmation prompt, **No** does not perform either of these two mutations. It also does not save any remaining form entries or reverse individual actions already carried out earlier.

Before **Yes**, check the project, existing assignments, and the intended action. Do not confirm again while a request is still running; there is no documented separate double-click protection here. If the response is unclear, first check the overview and the project again instead of blindly repeating the action.


## Delete project: stored data and local draft

**Warning: Deleting a saved project removes more than just the visible master data.** It also affects associated roles, shifts, staffing requirements, free assignment slots, applications/invitations, publications, and other project-related data. This is not archiving.

1. Check that you really want to delete the entire project, and choose **delete project**.
2. The prompt reads **Are you sure you want to delete this project?** With **No**, this deletion request does not proceed. Only **Yes** starts it.
3. If an assignment record with an employee ID greater than 0 is still linked via a project role, this deletion path is rejected — not only for confirmed assignments. Clarify the existing assignments with the administration; cancelling is not a harmless substitute to bypass this message.
4. Only a successful deletion response returns the view to the self-service project overview. In the case of a business error message, it remains open. After a technical or unclear response, have the actual status checked; partial changes are not guaranteed to be automatically rolled back.

The deletion scope also includes associated time/cancellation and rating data, payroll/expense and clothing data, messages, role-based notification records, validation and other project links. Messages already delivered are not recalled by this. Subprojects are not deleted with it: their link to the deleted parent project is cleared. Saved project templates are not deleted in this process. For the project image, before removing the file it is checked whether it is still referenced by an agency, customer, other project, or a template; this is not a blanket deletion of shared logos. Removing project-related billing/credit records does not imply any promised financial refund. This deletion path does not contain a separate employee dispatch job.

**Draft not yet saved on the server:** Without a project ID, after **Yes** the same button only tries to remove the matching local autosave entry and return to the overview; it does not send a project deletion to the server. In the tested version, however, the subsequent automatic saving when leaving can recreate the draft. Do not rely on permanent discarding here; check the draft list again. Contacts, departments, or locations already created separately are not undone by this local draft action.


## Cancel project: release assignments instead of deleting shifts

**Warning: Cancelling releases existing employee assignments and can trigger notifications.** Check the affected schedule before **Yes**. It is neither merely closing the view nor a draft step that can be reliably reversed.

Choose **cancel project** for the saved project. The German confirmation prompt can be translated as **The project will be deleted, all assignments will be cancelled and the corresponding employees will be notified. Continue?**. **This wording is not an exact description of the saved effects:** the tested process keeps the project, sets its type to cancelled, and prefixes the saved name with `C/`. Roles, shifts, and staffing requirements are not deleted. For existing assignments, the employee assignment is cleared and the assignment status is set to unassigned. Invitation/request records linked via project publications are permanently removed. A saved template is not changed by this.

**No** does not send a cancellation request. **Yes** starts the request regardless of the general **next**. The process is not limited to future assignments. Notifications are triggered before the subsequent changes: selected are people with requests linked to publications in the status assigned or completed. This is not a promise that every actually assigned person will be captured. This path uses a secured push job, not a separate email job. A successful response does not prove delivery; a failed push does not automatically prevent the subsequent cancellation.

After the response, this master data view remains without automatic refresh. The local success display does not reliably check the business response status. Therefore reopen the overview and the project and check the cancelled type, assignments, and requests; a visible translated project name does not have to show the new name prefix. Confirming again is not a safe retry method and can add the prefix again. For a local-only draft without a project ID, cancellation is not a usable discard button: it still attempts a server request and does not remove the local draft. In the case of errors or an unclear response, do not assume a complete rollback, restored invitations, or notifications sent.


## Error dialog while loading and OK for past data

If the dialog **Error!** appears while loading the master data, read the message before closing it. The x closes this dialog and then takes you to the start page, not automatically back to the project overview. This loading error disables the local automatic draft saving when leaving; closing is not a deletion, cancellation, or save confirmation. A missing edit permission may instead only block editing without opening this dialog. Not every technical issue is shown in exactly this dialog. If the error repeats, have the project and access checked instead of concluding from the error that planning was deleted.

The **OK** notice after selecting a past date is separate from this. It informs you that some functions do not support historical data. **OK** only confirms the notice: it does not save a project, remove a date, or trigger employee notification. The separate Yes/No question when saving newly added past data remains a different step; see [locations and assignment data](#edit-individual-days-multiple-selection-and-date-ranges). Shared Yes/No buttons always carry out the question currently shown, not a general "save project" action.


## Select project image and check upload

The image field is at the top of the project master data. Click in the upload area and choose an image file, or drag it in. The upload starts automatically after the file is accepted, not only with **next**. Upload files one after another and wait for feedback each time. The selection accepts the browser-reported type `image/*`, at most one file accepted at a time, and up to **50 MiB**. This is a selection check, not a promise that every image format can be processed server-side. The processing also tries to correct the image orientation.

**Before selecting, distinguish:**

| Situation | Effect of the upload |
| --- | --- |
| New project without a saved project ID | The file is uploaded already, but is initially stored only provisionally. Only successful project creation assigns the image path to the project. |
| Reopened existing project with ID | A successful upload immediately replaces the stored image path. **Back** does not reverse this image change; no further general save step is required for this upload. |

The image actions also use the permission **edit own projects**. The self-service edit permissions alone therefore do not explain every rejection. If the project is archived, if there is a pending order, or if editing is unavailable, the image area is blocked for pointer interaction. This is not general proof of a server-side status lock. A separate keyboard opener for the upload area is not documented here.

After a successful response, the image is displayed and the individual upload preview is removed from the queue. This disappearance is **not image deletion**. A preview alone proves neither the complete project save state nor a successful creation. If there is an error message, check file type, size, and permission; if the result is unclear, reopen a saved project before uploading again. Late responses can replace a display that has since changed; there is no reliable protection here against parallel image changes.

Temporary files are not a permanent draft store: a cleanup job takes files into account whose temporary entry is older than one day. This does not give you a guaranteed deletion time. Images still referenced by the agency, customer, project, or project template are protected during the tested cleanup. Therefore fully save any needed new project; local draft saving does not replace image assignment.


## Remove and enlarge project image

For a displayed image that is your own or not marked as an agency logo, **Delete** and **Enlarge** appear. A replacement image displayed as an agency logo does not have these two actions.

**Delete is a separate request, not a pending form change:**

1. Check the project and the image currently displayed. Click the trash icon **Delete**.
2. In **Confirmation**, “Do you really want to delete this?” appears. **No** closes without a removal request. **Yes** starts the removal immediately; do not click again preemptively.
3. If there is a saved image reference, a found project is switched to the agency logo. If there is no such match, a temporary image that is not referenced elsewhere is released for removal. Other references may prevent the physical file from being deleted. This is neither deleting the project nor deleting all copies of the image.
4. **Check especially carefully when images are shared:** The removal path searches via the image path, not uniquely via the open project. With multiple projects using the same image, another project reference may therefore be affected. Have administration review an unclear case instead of trying to correct it with more clicks.

If an agency logo returns, the display changes, but the previously local project image path is not reliably replaced in this response branch. **Reopen a saved project after the removal and check the image before saving any other changes with Next.** Otherwise, the old path may be sent again. Save or back up any required text changes **before removing the image**, not only between removal and reopening. An error response or missing update is not proof that the file remained unchanged; there is no guaranteed shared rollback of file and form.

**View only:** Click **Enlarge** to open the image in the large view. Clicking the image or its content area keeps the view open. Clicking the outer background or the close icon closes it. Opening and closing do not change either the image path or the project save state. No separate Escape close path is documented for this simple image view; it is not the same component as the other confirmation dialogs.


## Edit description: links, formatting, and character limit

Open the desired language tab and edit **Project description**. The tabs are called **English**, **Deutsch**, **Français**, and **Italiano**; switching between them alone does not save or translate anything. The following clarification supplements the 500-character limit described above: the plain text is counted without exactly the editor’s last line break, not the length of the saved HTML. Internal line breaks count; some characters such as emoji require more than one unit.

If the visible editor is changed, content that is too long is reset to the last accepted HTML content for that language, if necessary to empty. After a complete clear, the last accepted state can still be older text. This check is skipped for hidden language tabs. **The limit is therefore not a general server-side guarantee for loaded or automatically translated texts.** Check each required language visibly, shorten long texts before pasting, and then verify the actual content.

**Set or change a link:**

1. Select the text to be linked. Click the chain icon **HyperLink**; alternatively, Ctrl+K or the corresponding command key is provided in the editor. Without selected text, this insertion path does not open a new link dialog.
2. Enter the target address in the small link window and confirm its apply action or press **Enter**. **Escape** closes this link window without applying the newly entered target value. This does not yet save the whole project.
3. For an existing link, place the cursor in the link. The link window offers target preview, editing, and removal of the link. The target preview can open the external address in a new tab; check it before opening. Removing a link does not remove the text.

When plain text is pasted, recognized addresses are linked with `http://`, `https://`, or `www.`. Typed addresses are linked afterward when leaving the editor; existing links remain intact. `www.` receives `http://` as a prefix. Check the target address, especially when punctuation appears directly after an address. Protected spaces from pasted text are normalized.

The visible toolbar here contains **only the link button**, no image/video upload buttons and no full formatting palette. This does not mean “plain text only”: inserted formatting and the editor keyboard shortcuts for bold, italic, and underline are not excluded by this narrow toolbar alone. The description is stored as HTML.

A grayed-out editor is not reliable proof of a technical writing lock: the editor version used here does not implement the `disabled` set here as its own write-protection function; in addition, the form’s pointer overlay is active. Edit only projects that have been explicitly released. When leaving the editor, the local draft save for suitable new entries runs, not an automatic general server save process. For permanent saving, the rules for **Next** described above still apply.


## Translate name and description selectively

**Before translating:** The service sends the selected source text to the configured DeepL translation service. This is not a purely local browser function. Enter only content that is approved for this external translation path. This instruction does not promise a specific operating location, retention period, or error-free operation of the external service.

1. Open the **target tab**, for example **Deutsch**. If a name or description exists in another language, the magic-wand icon appears with the matching hint, such as **Translate into German**. While the translation is in progress, this button is disabled.
2. Check the source before clicking: the **first other populated language in the order English, German, French, Italian** is used automatically. A name **or** a description is already sufficient. The same source language is chosen for both fields, not the best available language for each one. Example: target German, English name available, description only in French — then the English name is used; the French description is not additionally used.
3. Click the magic wand once. A non-empty source field is translated and **replaces the corresponding target field even if text is already present there**. A truly empty source field does not start a request and leaves the target field as it is. This is not a “fill in missing translations only” button and not a translation of all tabs at the same time. Even HTML content that appears empty can count as populated.
4. Wait and check **name and description separately** in the target tab. They may be processed by two requests. The button returning after an error is not proof that both results are complete and correct. One part may already have been replaced while the other remains unchanged or arrives later.
5. Check name length, visible description, links, and meaning. Then save via the normal project path and verify the language versions after reopening.

**Do not correct the target text in the meantime and do not blindly translate again:** A delayed response can overwrite target text entered in the meantime. Switching tabs does not cancel the original request. The local error path removes the loading lock, but does not provide its own reliable field error message or restore the previous text. A repeated request can overlap with the other field request that is still running. If there is an error or the content remains unchanged, first check which fields have already been changed.

The translation request does not save any project changes. Its source-language selection is also **not** the priority used for later project saving: there, German still takes precedence over English, French, and Italian for the first existing name. For customer users, the main values can then be written into the account language. Preserve any required own translations before they are overwritten and check them after saving.


## Project type: click an existing match instead of Enter or Tab

The three selection lists **Project type**, **Project Manager**, and **department** each accept exactly one entry. The search filters the loaded names; it is not a promise that a missing match does not yet exist. Upper/lower case and accents are handled differently in the search than in the exact name check for a new project type.

For an existing **Project type**, click the match and then click the next field. **Tab does not select a match that is only highlighted**: it closes the list, but also triggers the type creation with the last accepted search text. That text may still be stored temporarily even after clicking or clearing. Therefore, do not continue with Tab from a just-filtered type selection if you do not want to create a new one.

**Enter** first accepts the highlighted match in the open picker and clears the search. When released, this empty search value is accepted first, then the creation handler runs into a technical error. In this event sequence, no additional type is requested; the already selected assignment remains. Do not rely either on a guaranteed harmless confirmation or on reliable creation of a new item with Enter when matches already exist. For an intended new creation, type the full name again, check the match, and use Tab once. If there is no response, do not repeat it: multiple requests are not blocked and a delayed response can replace a newer selection.

The **×** removes only the assignment in the form, not the type master record. For a new project, local draft saving is triggered; for a saved project, the type assignment is only changed permanently when the project is saved later. The separate type creation is distinct from this and may already have been saved beforehand.


## Project Manager: Enter and Tab can unexpectedly open KONTAKTDATEN

**Enter can also open the dialog **CONTACT DETAILS** after selecting an existing contact**. **Tab** opens it even when the search is empty; a highlighted match is not selected by Tab. Opening the dialog alone does not create a contact yet. For existing project managers, click the match and then click another field. Close an unwanted dialog with **Cancel**, not by confirming with Save.

After Enter on a highlighted match, the dialog opens with the first name cleared because selection happens first and the cleared search is then applied. With Tab, however, the dialog adopts the last cached search text. No check is made as to whether an existing contact has already been selected. After selecting or clearing, the visible search may be empty even though an earlier search text is still cached. Check **first name, last name, email, and mobile number completely**: the opener does not reliably clear the old contact fields. Automatic splitting affects only exactly two words separated by a space; other inputs can leave an old last name in place.

For a truly new contact, enter the name again, press Tab once, and check all dialog fields. Only saving requests the separate contact creation. Late responses can still replace a selection that has since changed; closing after submitting does not revoke the request. The **×** in the picker removes only the project assignment, deletes no contact, and sends nothing. The Project Manager remains a required field; after clearing, click a suitable existing contact again.


## Department: Enter is a creation action, not just a selection

**Enter can trigger both selection and department creation**: First the highlighted match is selected; for a saved project, this requests the department change. After that, the additional keyboard handler requests the last accepted search name as a new department. The later acceptance of the cleared search does not prevent this request. Unlike with project type, there is no check for an already existing entry with the same name before creation. A search prefix or text cached after selection/clearing can also become an unwanted new department. Do not press Enter multiple times.

For an existing **department**, click the match and then click another field. **Tab** closes the list without selecting the highlighted match and without creating a department of its own. For a truly new department, type the full name again, check existing entries, and use Enter once. If **Multiple departments in use** replaces the picker, that combined selection does not happen here.

For a saved project, selection alone already triggers its own change. If a successful creation response also arrives, its department is selected and a project change is requested again. These are separate processes, not an atomic action; the order of responses and write processing is not guaranteed. A failed second change does not roll back a department that has already been created. For new projects, the project assignment initially remains local, but the new department master record may still already have been saved.

The **×** and Backspace with an empty search can clear the assignment, but they do not delete a department master record. For a saved project, a change request follows immediately after that as well. After error messages or rapid changes, reopen and check the saved project and role state again. Archived or waiting projects disable these pickers; a mere editing overlay, by contrast, is not reliable keyboard or server-side rights protection.

## Cost center and department: selection and follow-up questions

For **cost center**, you can enter text or click an offered entry. The suggestions are filtered based on the entered text. The selection changes the cost center text here; it does not automatically reset the internal reference to another saved cost center. Check the assignment after saving if you switch between saved cost centers.

Select an existing **department** preferably by mouse click. This role list loads the project customer's departments and is not the same restricted list as in the project master data. A selection can therefore, after reloading, cause the role to no longer be visible for your user. Clearing it initially removes only the role assignment, not a department master record.

**Enter is not a harmless selection confirmation here:** In addition to the selection component, a department creation is linked to a cached search text. This can request a separate master record even before the role is saved; if the response is successful, the new department is assigned to the currently open role object. Avoid Enter to confirm existing matches. If an unintended creation occurs or a response arrives late, check the state instead of confirming repeatedly. A reliable keyboard-based new creation is not assumed for this dialog here.

When saving, the follow-up questions differ:

- **New creation without already loaded roles or editing of the only loaded role:** If cost center or department differs from the project, the transfer to the project is offered. **Yes** first requests the corresponding project changes and then continues saving the role, without waiting for the project responses. **No** also continues saving the role, but without these explicitly offered project requests; during new creation, the name is still checked.
- **Additional new creation or editing with multiple loaded roles:** Before values differ for the first time, another warning can appear. This also applies to an additional role when exactly one role is already loaded. **Yes** continues the role creation/change; **No** does not start a role request in this confirmation branch. Already changed local values are not reverted by this.

**Partial change despite an open role dialog:** When the offered project transfer is accepted, **Yes** starts the requests for a changed project cost center and then for a changed project department before the name check of the new role. If the name consists only of spaces, no role creation is requested; the dialog remains open. In that case, first check the project master data instead of blindly repeating **Save** and **Yes**. Neither the name error message nor the open dialog proves that the master data is untouched; conversely, requests already started are not confirmation of successful saving.

The number refers to the loaded roles; a list restricted by departments may hide other roles. A **No** response also does not mean that the project values remain unchanged permanently: when the role is saved server-side, the project department can be carried over automatically or cleared. The same applies to the cost center text when roles are changed. Empty cost centers and missing departments are omitted when determining common values. After that, check both roles **and** master data; there is no guaranteed shared transaction or rollback of all partial changes here.

## Do not confuse project template and role template

When starting from a **project template**, the included roles are created individually when switching to planning. This is an additional step after saving the project master data, not just a display of the template. After the role sequence, planning is reloaded. Until then, intermediate states may be visible; do not reload again too early or apply the same template a second time.

For these embedded roles, different default values apply than for a single role template: cost center references can come from the embedded role content, or alternatively from the new project. If the cost center has been explicitly changed for the transfer, these new values are used. The role department, by contrast, comes from the current project department, not from an unchanged role-specific copied template department. The previously described transfer of the project master data remains separate from this. This process also does not wait for every additional wage/expense/clothing request; it guarantees neither a complete shared transfer nor protection against repeated application.

## Copy a role to one or more locations

Open the desired project in Self-Service and the **planning** step. Expand the location and the relevant role. On the right in the role row is the copy icon with the hint **copy role**. The location selection appears only when the premium feature is enabled and the planning is not treated as archived or locked. The greyed-out editing area is not reliable proof of a complete keyboard or permission lock; if you lack editing rights, have the project managers perform the action.

## What happens to shifts and staffing requirements when you copy a role

A role copy is **not a complete copy of the staffing**. Before continuing with planning, check in particular:

- **Shifts and breaks:** The existing shift times, break duration, split breaks, as well as shift names and language texts, are created as new shifts. The copy is a new role with new shift records, not a move of the original role. The associated days are based on the project dates and the target location; no new project days are created here.
- **Staffing requirement and people:** Newly created staffing requirement starts at **0**, not with the quantity of the source role. Days without a matching date at the target location are not active. Personal assignments and invitations are not transferred to the copy. Therefore check the quantities and plan the staffing of the new role separately. The process reconciles staffing requirement and assignment slots across the whole project. **If there are excess assignment slots, this reconciliation can also remove existing assignments and related deployment, time, and billing data from other roles.** Not every copy leads to such deletions. Therefore clarify any unexplained discrepancies in quantities or staffing with the project owners before copying.
- **Role content:** Cost center text and cost center reference, as well as document references, are copied over. Existing salary details, expenses, and clothing regulations are transferred through separate copy steps; check these after loading instead of treating the copy as already fully verified. The documents are the same references, not a new upload.
- **Fields not fully transferred:** Employee filter, role department, and pay group are not transferred by this copy action as existing settings. The general role description is also not sent along, but its language texts are. The copy shown immediately may still display the source role's filter, pay group, or general description. Do not rely on that: reopen the planning after the responses and check these settings explicitly. A copy without a department can disappear from the visible role list for a customer user when reloading; in that case, have an authorized person check the department assignment instead of copying again blindly.

The project department can also be derived anew from the existing role departments when the copy is created. Empty role departments do not count as their own department. Therefore also check the project's basic data if departments are relevant to your planning. A later error does not guarantee that the already created role or shifts will be undone; always check the actual status before trying again.

Copied clothing images use the same stored image reference. The copy is not an independent image backup. If a role involved is deleted later, a deletion attempt is made for an image file. However, upload and display use different storage paths from the deletion routine; it is not proven whether this causes the displayed image of the other role to disappear. This also does not guarantee safety for all older storage states. Secure any needed images before deletion and then check the remaining role.


## Delete a role with its shifts

Open **Self-Service → Projects → desired project → Planning**, expand the location, and look for the **Delete** icon on the right in the relevant role row. This deletes the entire role, not just a single shift entry and not merely a reduction in staffing requirement.

1. Beforehand, check all shifts, staffing requirements, requests, and people already assigned to this role. If there are ongoing assignments, clarify the procedure with the project owners first.
2. Click **Delete**. The confirmation prompt reads **Are you sure you want to delete this role?** With **no**, no role deletion is started. With **yes**, the deletion request begins immediately; later saving the project is not necessary.
3. Wait for the response. Only then does the interface remove the role from its list. Afterwards, reopen the planning to check whether the role and related data were actually removed as expected.

**The confirmation covers more than the role name.** The deletion path removes the role's shifts, their staffing requirements, assignments and related requests, as well as role notifications, salary details, expenses, and clothing regulations. It does not replace a cancellation agreed with employees; deletion does not imply confirmed delivery of a notification.

A request with internal status `completed` on a shift blocks deletion with the English message **Cannot delete, you have a contract with at least one employee to work on this role!** This does not mean that every other already assigned or accepted request is automatically protected. The check is done shift by shift: if the blocking request is only found on a later shift, data from earlier shifts may already have been removed. **An error therefore does not reliably mean "nothing changed."** If there is a block or the outcome is unclear, do not delete again; instead reload and check the actual status with the project owners.

If exactly one **loaded** role remains in the project after a successful response, the interface copies its cost center into the project basic data and separately requests that it be saved, without any further prompt. Other roles may still exist because of department filters; this separate saving can also fail due to missing permissions even though the role has already been deleted. Therefore check the project cost center after reloading. The role deletion itself has no separate error callback here, and repeated confirmation or parallel changes to the role list are not reliably intercepted. Wait for completion and avoid further role actions in the meantime.

For previously copied clothing images, the remaining role copy is not an independent image backup: it uses the same stored image reference. The role deletion triggers a deletion attempt for an image file. However, upload and display use different storage paths from the deletion routine; an effect on the displayed image of the other role is therefore not proven. A general harmlessness for older storage states is also not proven. Secure such images beforehand and then check the remaining role.


## Create a shift in Self-Service planning

In **Self-Service**, open the project and step **2 – Planning**. Expand the appropriate location and role. Under the role, you will find **Add New Shift**. The shift belongs to this role; the date columns come from the project locations and dates. You do not select a single date when creating it. The visible roles may be limited by your customer departments.

1. Beforehand, check the role, location, and project data. Click **once** on **Add New Shift**. This creates the shift immediately, without any additional save or cancel dialog. **Continue** is not required for this.
2. Wait until the new row appears. For the first loaded shift of the role, **08:00–16:00** is preset. If shifts already exist, the start **and** end are initially set to the end of the last loaded shift. Matching times are therefore not yet a fully planned assignment.
3. Check the times, break time, and every required date column. Newly created staffing requirement fields start at **0**; existing staffing quantities in the project are not generally reset. Only dates that belong to this location are intended as active staffing fields. A new shift does not assign a person yet and does not send an invitation through this action.

The initial break time is determined from the shift duration: in Self-Service, a matching customer break rule takes precedence. If no customer rule matches this duration, the agency rules apply; without a matching calculation, **0** may result. Do not assume a fixed standard break.

**Clarify any unexplained quantity/staffing discrepancies in the project before creating it:** this action also reconciles assignment slots across the whole project. If there are excess slots, assignments as well as deployment, time, and billing data from other roles can be removed. This does not necessarily happen with every creation, but it is not solely a local change to the new row.

If there is no response or the response is unclear, do not click multiple times. Ongoing creations are not blocked against each other and can create additional shifts. Wait for the response, reload the planning, and check location, role, and shift inventory before acting again. If you lack edit permissions or the planning is locked, have the project owners make the change; greyed-out areas alone do not prove a complete keyboard lockout.


## Change times and simple break time

Open **Self-Service → Project → 2 – Planning**, expand the location and role, and find the shift row. On the left, start and end are shown in the format **HH:MM**, next to **break time** in minutes. These times belong to the shift and therefore apply to its staffing needs across multiple project days, not just to a single date column.

1. Enter a complete time from **00:00 to 23:59**. The full entry is checked, but it does not save on its own yet. When **leaving the time field or pressing Enter**, the break is recalculated and the shift is then saved. Enter can trigger this without leaving the time field; both actions can also save with unchanged valid values. Repeated Enter presses and then leaving the field can start additional calculations and saves.
2. An incomplete or invalid entry, such as `10:__` or `24:00`, is rejected with **"Invalid time. Please enter a time in the format HH:MM (00:00 - 23:59)."** The field is reset to the value it had when you entered it. If another save was already open at that time, this reset is not proof of the last saved server value.
3. For a night shift, you can use **22:00–06:00**, for example; an earlier end is treated as ending after midnight. When changing it, such a span is rejected if it exceeds 23 hours. Identical start and end times are treated as a duration of **0** for break calculation, not automatically as 24 hours. Check the desired period yourself.
4. For a simple break without split breaks, enter the minutes in the **break time** field and leave the field. This starts saving immediately, without a shared save button. The field allows up to three characters, but it does not provide reliable validation of whole, non-negative minutes or a duration suitable for the shift. Use sensible whole minutes and check after reloading. If split breaks are stored, this simple field is disabled; their separate operation is not described here.

**Time changes recalculate the break again.** A break entered manually beforehand can therefore be replaced. Do not change start, end, and break in quick succession: calculations and save responses are not protected against delayed responses. An older break calculation can be saved together with the times that have since been entered. If the result is unclear, wait for all responses first, then reload and check the three values together.

For certain assignments that are already in progress or being edited, the server rejects a time change. This includes existing clock-ins and assignments with validated, in-progress, or completed time editing; for confirmed or self-booked assignments, an additional date/time-based pre-start lock applies. The message is **"The times for this shift cannot be changed because at least one of the assignments for this shift has already started."** Start, end, and break are then reset to the values returned. This lock is checked when times are changed; it is not an equivalent promise for a pure break change. Clarify such corrections with the project owners instead of trying to bypass the lock.

When saving the break directly, **"Shift could not be changed. Please try again!"** may appear; the local input is not automatically reset. Even if there is no response after a time change, do not assume the save was successful. Reload first and check the actual status. A successful time change is also not proof that all affected people were notified. Coordinate relevant changes separately.


## Enter the staffing requirement per shift and date

Go to **Self-Service → Project → 2 – Planning**, expand the location and role, and find the appropriate **date column** to the right of the desired shift. The number field is the regular staffing requirement; a separately displayed overbooking next to it is not part of the visible field value. Dates that are not active at the location are disabled. Therefore first check the shift, date, and location.

**Each entry starts its own save immediately**, not only when leaving the field or by using **Next**. There is no cancel dialog here and no shared final save. Even temporary clearing while replacing a number is transmitted as regular staffing requirement **0**. Therefore replace quantities deliberately and wait for the response before changing anything else.

Enter a sensible **whole, non-negative number of people**. The number field and the bound save path do not guarantee full validation of whole numbers and sensible upper limits. Do not use negative or decimal entries as a supported type of staffing requirement. The saved total value includes regular staffing requirement plus existing overbooking; this is not a personal commitment or automatic staffing. The separate overbooking control is not described here.

If you reduce the staffing requirement below the already assigned slots, the application can keep the total staffing requirement and show the difference as overbooking. Example with previously **5 regular slots**, no overbooking, and **4 assigned slots**: a new regular staffing requirement of **1** can come back as **1 regular + 3 overbooked**. Therefore always check both displays and the staffing. **0 in the regular field does not necessarily mean total staffing requirement 0 when there is existing overbooking or assignment.**

If the total staffing requirement actually reaches 0, open invitations with status `pending` are quietly removed in the corresponding save branch. This is neither a personal cancellation notification nor the same action as closing an assignment. A later increase does not automatically restore the removed invitations; check the invitation list afterwards.

**Clarify any unresolved assignments and time/billing data before changing quantities.** The subsequent slot reconciliation affects the whole project. If there are excess assignment slots, it can also remove assignments and assignment, time, and billing data from other roles. The overbooking adjustment described here is therefore not a general guarantee that all data will remain unchanged.

With **"The number of employees could not be changed. Please try again!"**, the local entry may remain visible. Even rapid successive changes are not reliably confirmed by the last visible response: an older response can overwrite the new display; numeric zero values from a response are not always adopted. Wait for open responses, reload, and check regular staffing requirement, overbooking, and people together before entering anything again. If values conflict, involve the project owners.


## Remove a single shift

Open **Self-Service → Project → 2 – Planning**, expand the location and role, and look for the delete icon with the label **Delete** on the right in the **shift row**. Do not confuse it with the delete icon for the entire role. Here you remove the shift with its staffing requirements across the associated project days, not just a single staffing requirement on one date.

1. Beforehand, check the affected dates, invitations, assignments, and already recorded times. Coordinate any necessary cancellations separately.
2. Click the shift delete icon. The prompt reads **"Are you sure you want to delete this shift?"**. **No** does not trigger deletion; **Yes** requests deletion immediately. Later shared project saving is not necessary.
3. Wait for the response and, after reloading, check the correct role. If successful, the shift, its staffing requirements, and assignments are removed; related requests are deleted. This does not imply confirmed notification of the affected people or guaranteed complete cleanup of all dependent time/billing data.

The message **"A shift with invitations cannot be deleted!"** is worded more broadly than the checked lock: this single action blocks on related requests with status `completed` or `auto-accepted`, not generally on every existing invitation. Therefore, a missing lock does not mean the shift is unstaffed or can be deleted without consequences. If deletion is refused, do not first remove invitations just to bypass the lock; clarify the assignment with the project owners.

Do not click **Yes** multiple times and do not change the affected shift list while the response is open. After a successful response, the display removes a previously remembered row position; if the list has since been reordered, the wrong row may be removed temporarily. If there is an error, an unclear response, or an unexpected display, reload first and check what actually exists. The action is not intended when planning is locked and requires appropriate Self-Service edit rights at the shift endpoint; this is not a general security approval for all planning actions.


## Edit overbookings for a shift and a day

1. Open **Self-Service → Project → Step 2 (Planning)** and expand the relevant location and role. Find the shift row and the date column. Next to the regular staffing requirement there is **+**, and for positive overbooking its number. The label is **Add overbooking** or **Edit overbooking**. The entry point appears only for the matching active staffing requirement once its loading process is complete.
2. Click this symbol and change **Number of overbookings**. This means additional places on top of the regular requirement, not a new regular staffing requirement number. Example: with 5 regular places and 2 overbookings, a total of 7 places are planned. Changing to 3 overbookings results in a total of 8 places if the regular requirement stays the same.
3. Click **Save** to send the change immediately. The dialog closes before the response arrives. A later **Next** or shared project save is not required for this. Afterward, check both the regular requirement and the overbooking, as well as the assigned people for exactly this shift and this day.

**Typing is not yet saving:** Entering a value and leaving the number field first only changes the dialog value. Enter in the number field is not a save command here; use **Save**. **Cancel** or the close symbol at the top closes without saving a new overbooking. When reopened, the then current value of the staffing requirement cell is taken over. Escape and clicking outside do not replace the close symbol in this dialog. Cancel does not undo a saving that has already been sent.

**Enter valid quantities deliberately:** Use a non-negative whole number; **0** removes the additional overbooking, not the regular requirement automatically. This is an operating recommendation, not a guaranteed quantity check. An emptied field is not treated as “keep the old number”: saving sends an empty overbooking value, while the total amount is recalculated. Negative numbers or decimal values are also not reliably caught in the save handler. Do not save such values. If the total amount is unexpected, reload first and check both quantities; already loaded values and responses are not consistently converted to numbers.

**People already assigned can prevent a reduction:** If you reduce the overbooking and the new total would be smaller than the number of assignments with a status other than “unassigned”, this branch does not take over the new quantities, but returns the previous ones. Example: 5 regular places plus 2 overbookings, already 6 assigned → setting overbooking to 0 would result in 5 in total; this reduction branch therefore keeps the previous 7/2. It still performs the project-wide quantity reconciliation. The dialog may be closed without a special rejection message; that alone does not confirm the new quantity. The behavior differs from a reduction in the regular requirement field, which can absorb assigned places by increasing the overbooking.

**Check project-wide effects in advance:** This save also reconciles assignment places across the entire project, not just in the opened cell. If the requirement and assignment quantities conflict, other roles may also be affected. Depending on the branch, excess assignments and related time, expense, and billing data may be removed; there is no general promise of retention or undo here. Clarify such discrepancies with the responsible person before changing quantities. The overbooking number alone is neither a personal assignment nor a confirmed commitment.

If, in the normal save branch, a total amount of 0 with overbooking 0 is actually saved, then pending invitations with status “pending” for this requirement are removed afterward and project counters are recalculated. Simply setting the overbooking to 0 is not enough if the regular requirement remains positive. This is not a guaranteed personal cancellation notice and not a general closing of the shift. A later increase does not automatically restore the removed invitations.

**In case of errors or rapid follow-up changes:** The message **The number of employees could not be changed. Please try again!** does not reset the local quantities and does not reopen the dialog. Repeated saving can trigger additional operations, even without changing the value. An older response can overwrite a newer display; a numeric zero in the response is also handled differently from the string “0”. This describes possible response constellations, not the proven data type of your system. While the dialog is open, responses can also change the underlying requirement; saving then recalculates from this now current requirement and the dialog value. Wait for feedback, reload in a controlled way if the result is unclear, and check quantities and people before changing anything again.

**Observe archiving and permissions:** A locked planning is not an approved editing path. The quantity field in the dialog is disabled for archived projects; however, this does not imply a general lock guarantee for every button, keyboard action, or server request. Do not try to bypass a lock; instead clarify the editing authorization. This guide concerns overbookings only; split breaks as well as shift names and languages are not validated here.


## Split breaks: open the dialog and edit time ranges

Open your project and its **planning** in **Self-Service**. At the relevant location, find the desired role and shift. Next to the break duration field, click the small break button. The dialog is called **"Split shift - break(s)"**. It first loads the existing breaks for this shift. The shift belongs to the role, not just to a single day cell: therefore also check the affected assignment days. Depending on customer access and department filter, you may only see part of the roles.

**Add** creates an empty row. Enter the break start on the left and the break end on the right in **HH:MM** format, for example **12:00 – 12:30**. This means the break between work segments, not an additional work block. Use the remove button on the right to take a row out of the dialog. Adding, entering, and removing do not send a save request on their own yet. The Add button is disabled when four rows already exist and for archived projects; this is not a general server-side limitation or security assurance.

**Enter in the break time field does not save.** Complete entries and leaving a time field trigger its time validation; Enter also calls this check, without necessarily moving the focus on. Invalid or incomplete times may be cleared. Check both fields again before saving. This is a different process from directly editing the shift start and shift end time outside this dialog.

**Cancel** and the dialog’s own close button at the top do not save the current dialog changes. When reopened, the breaks are loaded again. However, they do not undo a save that has already been started. Escape is not a reliable way to close the dialog: in the time field it can reset the entry there; the dialog itself is not set up to close via Escape or clicking outside. Use the visible buttons.


## Saving split breaks: time frame, night, and total break

All breaks need a start and an end and must lie within the shift. A break’s end must be later than its start. Breaks must not overlap; directly adjacent time ranges are not rejected as overlaps. For a shift that crosses midnight, times after midnight are assigned to the following day, for example a break **23:30 – 00:30** within a **22:00 – 06:00** shift. The dialog check treats identical start and end times for the entire shift as a day change, not as an empty time span. Do not use this special case as a substitute for a substantive check of your shift duration.

Click **Save** only after checking. The breaks list is sent immediately for the shift; **Next** or saving the entire project later is not required for this. Missing times, an unsuitable time frame, or overlaps prevent the request in the checked dialog flow. The hints may appear in English, for example **„Split shifts must not overlap.“**. Correct the affected rows and only then save again. The input form and these checks are not complete server-side validation and not an approval under labor law.

After the response to the breaks list, the entire shift is additionally saved with a calculated total break. The dialog closes and reports **„Split shift saved!“** without waiting for the response to this second save. The message therefore does not confirm that both steps completed successfully. After reloading in a controlled way, open the shift again and check both the break times and the total break duration.

**Important for night breaks and when removing all rows:** The subsequent summation only takes into account breaks whose end time is later than their start time. A break that runs past midnight can pass the time-frame check, but be missing from this sum. If the calculated sum is zero or the list is empty, the previous total break duration is retained instead of being automatically set to zero. In a mixed case, only the same-day part can become part of the total break. Do not rely solely on the checkmark or the success message here. If there are discrepancies, clarify them with your responsible planner before further mission or time processing.

As long as the loaded shift contains break rows, the simple break-duration field next to it is disabled. After all rows have been removed and saved, it becomes available again; check and correct the remaining total break separately if needed. The tooltip when hovering over the break button shows the resulting **work segments**, not simply the list of break times. It uses the local state and is not a fresh fetch or a reliable proof of the correct night order.


## Split breaks: errors, parallel changes, and follow-up data

**Do not save an unexpectedly empty list right away.** Even after a loading error, the dialog may appear empty. Saving an empty list removes the previous break entries for this shift. Close the dialog, reload in a controlled way, and check the previous state before confirming changes. When saving, the transmitted list is reconciled: missing previous rows are removed. In case of errors, no reliable joint rollback of the breaks list and the subsequent shift save is documented.

Edit only one break dialog at a time if possible and wait for ongoing requests to finish. Repeated saving is not prevented as a duplicate request. If you open another shift before a response arrives, an older loading response can overwrite that dialog’s list. An older save response can locally change the shift that is now selected, trigger another shift save for it, and close the newer dialog. **Cancel** also does not stop older requests. If the display is delayed or contradictory, do not click Save repeatedly; instead reload in a controlled way after completion and check all shifts that were edited in the meantime.

If the dialog remains open after saving or an error message appears, check the state before trying again. The dialog does not always show loading and saving errors itself; a general error message may also appear. Even a closed dialog is not complete proof of success. The follow-up save sends the entire local shift, not just its break duration. Therefore be especially careful if shift times or other shift data were changed in the meantime.

Break planning does not replace correction of already recorded working times, wages, or expenses. No automatic complete follow-up of such data is evidenced from this save path. For assignments already carried out, check the time and billing data separately with the responsible office. The permission check of the subsequent shift save must also not be understood as a guarantee of protection for the preceding break fetch or break write. Use only the project access intended for you; greyed-out interfaces are not a comprehensive security clearance.

This guide concerns only the Self-Service planning’s own break dialog. Shift names, language editing, and other planning dialogs are not covered by this.
