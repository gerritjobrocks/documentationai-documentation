## Open the profile and check the notes

1. Open the side menu and select **Profile**.
2. Read the note below your profile picture. In the view shown, the app warns that no job invitations will be received if the profile is incomplete.
3. Open the individual expandable sections and check the fields marked with an asterisk.

The following English field names correspond to the screenshots provided. Red section borders and the warning help with orientation; check the individual required fields instead of concluding from color alone that a record has been fully verified. A save or confirmation process is not visible in the screenshots. This guide therefore neither promises automatic saving nor successful verification of your details or uploads.

## Person Details – personal details

Here, **Last Name**, **First Name**, **Nickname**, **Gender**, **Smoker**, **Date of Birth**, **Age** and **No. of Children** are visible. There is also a **Video-Selfportrait** field for a short video introduction.

Check the name and date of birth in particular. The view shows an age field, but does not indicate whether it is calculated automatically or maintained manually.

## Appearance – appearance and sizes

This section shows height, eye and hair color, as well as shoe, shirt and trouser sizes. It also includes **Visible Tattoos**, **Visible Piercings** and a description of the piercings.

Enter the details your organization needs for your profile. From this view it cannot be determined which details are required for every assignment.

## Images – profile pictures

There are six image slots. **Image 1 (Portrait)** and **Image 2 (Full Body)** are marked with an asterisk; the other slots are called **Image 3** through **Image 6**. A trash can icon is visible in the filled portrait field.

Use the correspondingly labeled upload fields for portrait and full-body image. File formats, size limits and the procedure for a deletion confirmation are not shown in these screenshots.

## Skills – CV and qualifications

This section contains:

- **Upload CV:** upload CV; the field is marked with an asterisk.
- **Education:** education, with **Add new**.
- **Professional Experience:** work experience, also with **Add new**.
- **Languages:** language (**Name**) and level (**Skill Level**) for each entry.
- **Upload Certificate:** upload certificate.

For language skills, check both the language and the assigned level. The forms behind **Add new** are not shown; required fields for these follow-up steps are not assumed here.

## Address & Contact Information – address and contact details

In the address section, the main and secondary address as well as the **legal address** designation are visible. The fields shown include address line, street, house number, postal code, city, country, canton and **Valid from**.

In the contact section you will find landline (**Fix**), mobile number (**Mobile**), email and **Recovery Email**. The **Emergency Contact** section has fields for first name, last name, phone number and email.

Under **Socials** are Web, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and Skype. Only enter data that you want to provide in your profile or that is required as mandatory information. The screenshots do not prove that other people have access rights to this data.

## General Availability – availability and absences

This section separates two inputs:

1. **Unavailability Period:** absence periods with **Add new** and a list with **Start Date** and **End Date**. The empty list shown says **No absence dates stored!**
2. **Availability:** a weekly/hourly grid, the options **only evenings** and **only weekends**, as well as **see all**.

Check both your general time windows and specific absence periods. An empty absence list and marked grid fields are different pieces of information. The screenshots show neither the dialog for adding a period nor the effect of a change on assignments already in place. A change here is therefore not to be understood as a cancellation of an assignment that has already been confirmed.

## Work Information – work and ID data

The fields shown include **SSN**, **SSN Card Upload**, **Marital Status**, **Valid from**, **Nationality**, ID/passport uploads for front and back, and **Work Permit**. Several of these are marked with an asterisk.

In the same section, **Generate Payslip** is visible with a selection. The example view reports **No payslip for the selected month**. This message refers to the selected month; it confirms neither a payslip that has already been created nor that a payment has been received.

Submit ID and social security data only through the intended profile area. If you are unsure about the required documents, ask your responsible organization; do not send such documents in freely accessible comments.

## Bank Information – bank details

Visible are **IBAN** (with an asterisk), **Account Type**, **Bank Name**, **Bank Location**, **Clearing Number** and **Account Number**.

Check the details carefully. The visible input form alone does not prove successful bank account verification or a payout.

## Transportation Information – mobility

This section contains selection fields for:

- **Public Transportation Subscription:** for example no subscription, half-fare, GA or regional network.
- **Driver Licence Type:** no driver’s license or the driver’s license categories shown.
- **Car Availability:** own car, car in household or no car available.
- **Car Sharing Subscription:** the listed providers or **other**.

Only select information that applies to you. A subscription, a driver’s license and an available car are separate pieces of information.

## Interests and Further documents

**Interests** contains checkboxes for product areas and types of work, for example IT, food, promotion, service, kitchen, security, care and cleaning. Interests are not automatically proof of a qualification.

**Further documents** offers **Add new**. Which document types and follow-up fields are offered is not shown.

At the end of the profile overview, **Delete Profile** is also visible. The screenshots show neither a confirmation dialog nor the consequences of this action. Do not use it as a substitute for logging out or correcting individual details.

## Before leaving the profile

Check required-field notes and visible feedback from the app. If it remains unclear whether information was applied, open the relevant section again and check the displayed status. This is not a substitute for confirmation from the organization for verified documents or payroll data.
