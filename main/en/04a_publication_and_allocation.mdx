title: "Publication and assignment"

Step **3** determines whether you publish jobs or proceed to staffing without publication. Both are active actions. You need a saved, editable project and a checked plan with locations, days, roles, shifts, requirements and role filters.

## Distinguish between four processes

| Process | Purpose | Do not confuse with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Registration** | A person registers via your agency's link. | Not an application for a specific assignment. |
| **Activation** | The employee status in the pool is enabled for the further assignment process. | No confirmed shift yet; a configured automatic invitation is a separate follow-up process. |
| **Assignment request / publication** | A job offer is made available to suitable existing employees. | Still no confirmed staffing and no proof of a delivered push message. |
| **Assignment / self-booking** | A specific person is assigned to a specific shift, or books themselves in according to the project settings. | Not done by creating a role, entering a requirement or registering. |

For new employees, see [Find registration link](/main/en/05_employees_dashboard#invite-employees-to-register).

## Publish jobs now

1. Open step **3** in the project after you have checked the [planning](/main/en/04_project_planning).
2. Use **Send push notifications** to decide whether employees should also receive a push message. **Turned off does not mean unpublished:** job offers can be published without push; in that case, the subject and message are ignored according to the notice.
3. Leave **resend notifications** turned off unless you also want already invited employees to be notified again intentionally. The switch is only available when push is enabled. Turning off push also turns off the resend notification.
4. When push is enabled, fill in **Subject** and **message** in the desired language tabs. Both are required. The subject allows a maximum of **50 characters**; for the message text, the interface shows a counter for **256 characters**. Check the texts as a whole for each language.
5. If needed, use the available placeholders for first name, last name, project name, role, customer and location. In Self Service, the customer placeholder is not offered. Also check loaded notification templates before sending.
6. Check the **Deadline** field separately. This date is neither the assignment day nor the cancellation deadline from step 1. Leave **Scheduled publication** turned off for immediate publication.
7. Click **Publish jobs**, read the confirmation and only confirm after checking the desired sending behavior.

The interface initializes push as on, resend as off, and scheduling as off. Do not rely on this in a project that has already been edited; check the visible switches.

## Schedule publication for later

1. Prepare the switches, subject, message and, if applicable, deadline as above.
2. Activate **Scheduled publication**.
3. Set the additionally displayed **date** and **time** for the publication, not for the work assignment.
4. Click **Publish jobs** and confirm. This path schedules a publication instead of publishing immediately.
5. After a successful response, step 4 opens. This page change is not proof of sending. If it is unclear whether the scheduled time was saved, have support check the scheduled publication before triggering it again.

## Assign without publication

If you want to move to staffing without publication:

1. First check role filters, shifts and requirements in step 2.
2. Open step **3** and click **Assign without publication** if the action is enabled for your access.
3. Read the confirmation and only confirm if this processing is intended.
4. After a successful response, step **4** opens. The interface indicates **background processing**; the list updates automatically.
5. Wait for the result and then check the specific people and shifts in staffing. A panel that is initially empty is not a reason to trigger the action again immediately.

**Assigning without publication is not saving a draft and not a harmless page change.** It triggers background processing. However, successful acceptance of the action does not yet prove a confirmed shift assignment for a specific person. Check the actual status in step 4.

## Assess success and errors correctly

You can find the detailed guide to candidate status, assignments, conflicts, cancellations and preparation mode under [staffing in step 4](/main/en/04b_staff_allocation).

- **After immediate publication or assigning without publication:** check step 4 and the updated assignment/person statuses. Processing may still be running after the page change.
- **Notification history:** In step 3 you can see saved actions. An entry alone confirms neither a publication that has already been carried out nor delivery of a message: a scheduled publication or **assign without publication** can also already create an entry. Also check the actual status in step 4; have scheduled or unclear actions clarified before triggering them again.
- **Subject or message missing:** If push is enabled, add the corresponding language version. Do not simply turn off push to bypass an intended message without content.
- **No suitable people:** Check the role filters and the requirement, and in Self Service also the customer pool. Registration alone does not yet mean eligibility for assignments or an application.
- **Unclear result / connection error:** First check the project, history and assignment again. Do not blindly publish again or activate **resend notifications**.
- **Step or action locked:** Clarify editing rights, archiving, and project context with the administrator. Self Service remains in its own workflow with the associated rights.
